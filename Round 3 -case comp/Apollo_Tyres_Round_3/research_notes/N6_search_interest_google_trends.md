# N6. Search interest in tyre brands before and after the Team India deal (Google Trends)

Pulled 6 November 2026 in the main session, from Google Trends' own data for India. Research only.

## Why this was done

The case brief says the effect of the jersey on farmers is "untested". Search interest is a free, public proxy for consideration. It lets us ask two things: who leads when people search for a tractor tyre by brand, and whether Apollo's position changed after the sponsorship began on 16 September 2025.

## Method

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| Source | [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=2024-09-01%202026-11-05&geo=IN&q=apollo%20tractor%20tyre,mrf%20tractor%20tyre,ceat%20tractor%20tyre,bkt%20tractor%20tyre,goodyear%20tractor%20tyre&hl=en), India, web search |
| Period | 1 September 2024 to 5 November 2026 |
| "Before" | 1 September 2024 to 15 September 2025 (55 weeks) |
| "After" | 16 September 2025 to 5 November 2026 (59 weeks) |
| Tractor-tyre terms | For each of Apollo, MRF, CEAT, BKT and Goodyear: "[brand] tractor tyre + [brand] tractor tyres + [brand] tractor tire + [brand] tyre tractor" (the plus sign means "or"). A first run with only "[brand] tractor tyre" gave the same picture. |
| Brand terms | "apollo tyres", "mrf tyres", "ceat tyres", "jk tyre", "bkt tyres", restricted to the Autos and Vehicles category to cut out share-price searches |
| State figures | Google's "compared breakdown by subregion": for each state, the five terms' shares of searches, summing to 100 |
| National figures | Average of the weekly index (0 to 100, relative to the highest point among the five terms) |

All figures below are [Outside-in proxy; single source]. Averages and shares of averages are [Computed].

### What it shows

- **MRF leads tractor-tyre search in every large tractor state**, with 40 to 55% of the five-brand total. [Outside-in proxy]
- **BKT is second in most of them**, at 17 to 27%, and strongest in Punjab and Haryana. [Outside-in proxy]
- **Apollo holds 8 to 18%**, third or fourth. Punjab is its weakest of the eight states (8 to 9%). [Outside-in proxy]
- **Apollo's share did not move after the jersey deal.** The eight-state average went from 14.0 to 14.8. It fell in Uttar Pradesh, Punjab and Maharashtra, was flat in Rajasthan and Gujarat, and rose in Haryana, Madhya Pradesh and Bihar. Changes of this size are within the noise of low-volume search data. [Interpretation]

### What it shows

- Searches for "apollo tyres" rose a little in the two quarters around the announcement (17.3 and 18.0 against 15.4 to 16.7 before) and then fell below their earlier level. [Outside-in proxy]
- There is no sustained rise in search interest in Apollo in the main tractor states after the sponsorship began. [Interpretation]

## Limits

- Search interest is a proxy for consideration, not a measure of recall or sales. People who search are younger and more online than the average tractor owner.
- Google Trends reports samples and relative indices, not counts. Low-volume terms are noisy and are sometimes shown as zero.
- Terms are in English and Roman script. Searches in Hindi, Punjabi or Marathi script, and searches by product name (Shakti, Krishak, Virat, Commander), are not counted.
- A sponsorship can raise familiarity without raising search. The absence of a search lift is evidence against a strong effect on active consideration, not proof of no effect.
- The comparison is before and after, with no control. It cannot separate the sponsorship from the GST cut, price changes or rivals' activity.

## Use in the analysis

- As the first public data point on whether the jersey has moved tractor-tyre consideration: it has not, visibly.
- As evidence of the size of MRF's lead, and of BKT's strength in Punjab and Haryana, where the first pilot belt was proposed.
- As a cheap tracking measure Apollo can repeat every quarter.
