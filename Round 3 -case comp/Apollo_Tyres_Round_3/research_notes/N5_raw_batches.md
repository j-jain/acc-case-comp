### McKinsey - sports sponsorship (secondary copy opened: Birdwell blog, 8 Dec 2014, https://paul-birdwell.squarespace.com/rf-blog/2014/12/8/...). Authors per snippet: Jeff Jacobs, Pallav Jain, Kushan Surana, June 2014.
Five metrics as relayed: (1) cost per reach (retailer: 15% of properties cost twice average; reallocating raised reach 20% at same cost); (2) unaided awareness per reach (USD 0.50-1.60 activation per USD 1 rights; a CPG firm at 80/20 rights/activation lifted unaided awareness 15% by shifting to activation); (3) sales/margin per dollar (handset maker: tenfold ROI gap top vs bottom quartile); (4) long-term brand attributes (brand strength 60-80% of sales; some sponsorships negative ROI due to poor fit); (5) indirect benefits (financial institution golf sponsorship covered its cost through indirect wealth-management sales). [Single source, secondary]
