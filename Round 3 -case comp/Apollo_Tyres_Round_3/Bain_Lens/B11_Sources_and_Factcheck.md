# B11. Sources and the fact-check

Prepared 7 November 2026. Part of the Bain-lens re-analysis of the Apollo Round 3 case.

**What this file is.** The register of every Bain page the Bain lens relies on, with its date and what was taken from it; a map of the other sources by research note; the round-6 fact-check of the B files; and the audits run on the folder. The McKinsey-lens register is `..\17_Source_Register_and_Verification.md` sections 11 to 14 and is not repeated.

**Rules applied.**

1. Anything attributed to Bain comes from a bain.com page or a Bain book excerpt on bain.com, fetched and read on 7 November 2026; quotes are verbatim; dates are as printed.
2. Nothing in this folder says or implies that Bain advised Apollo or wrote any of this. Every file carries the sentence "It is not Bain's work."
3. Where a first reading of a page was wrong, the correction is recorded (section 1.3) and applied.
4. Secondary descriptions of Bain's method (coaching sites, former consultants) are labelled as such and never attributed to Bain (`..\research_notes\Q1_bain_method_sources.md` section 3).

## 3. Fact-check, round 6

Run on 7 November 2026 on the B02 to B08 files as first written; the full table, with the source URL and the exact wording found for each claim, is `..\research_notes\R_factcheck_round6.md`. Thirty-five load-bearing external claims were read at the primary source (bain.com pages and PDFs, CRISIL and CARE rationales, HDFC Securities notes, the Apollo call transcripts, the CCI orders and two consumer-court judgments on indiankanoon, five makers' warranty pages, the ICC and BCCI schedule pages, the Frontiers paper, J.D. Power's release, the Tribune, YouGov, the agency and vendor case studies, and the two tractor portals).

| Verdict | Count | What it covered |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | 25 | Every verbatim Bain quote (10 of 10); CRISIL's industry and Apollo figures; Goodyear's share, decline and the retain decision; the warranty terms of Apollo, CEAT and Goodyear; the two court cases; J.D. Power; Mahindra Shree; the BCCI schedule; the Rural Mobile Store and AVOLVE; the Q4 FY26 call; the trailer-share quote; JK Advantage; the masons' case; Fevicol |
| Confirmed with a correction | 8 | The CCI 2012 record (attribution: company submissions and a dissent, not a Commission finding); the 2018 penalty (fined, not paid; remanded in December 2022); the Q3 FY26 quote (spliced; now two sentences); YouGov's 17.2% (RCB-specific); the Haryana tournaments (two villages fused); the NSSO figures (farmers' organisations, not FPOs; Indo-Gangetic, not northern); Castrol's 1.27 lakh (citation re-pointed); the portal dealer count (18 pages with repeats, not 12) |
| Partly confirmed | 2 | FY26 EBITDA margins (vary by up to half a point by source; BKT's includes other income); Hero's rural share (42-43%, not 40%; the 500 executives and "about half" not verified at source) |
| Not confirmed | 0 | – |
| Could not check | 0 | – |

No figure was found to be invented; the errors were of attribution and quote-splicing. The eleven corrections listed in R were applied on 7 November 2026 to B00, B02, B03, B04, B05, B06, B08, B09 and to Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q7. The Bain quotes and the arithmetic of B07 were not touched by any correction.

**Claims the round did not reach.** The B07 cases and sensitivities (our own arithmetic on M07's inputs, not external claims); the BARC and streaming viewership figures in Q7, most of which are broadcaster claims via press and are labelled so; the remaining benchmarks in Q5 (Asian Paints, Berger, Ambuja, UltraTech, Shell, Amaron, Coromandel, Bayer), which carry their company-page or press labels from the round-6 research.
