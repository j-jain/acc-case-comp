## 1. State-wise tractor sales (latest year), state shares, newer stock data, HP mix by region

### Takeaway
Full-year FY26 VAHAN retail by state is available only for the top five states (UP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, MP, Gujarat; together about 62% of the national 10.31 lakh). UP and Maharashtra are essentially tied for first (rank flips by period), Rajasthan third. Punjab and Haryana are not top-volume states but are the high-HP states. No state-wise tractor population newer than the 2012 IFPRI figure was found.

### Cited Findings
- FY26 (Apr 2025 to Mar 2026) retail registrations: national 10,30,895; Uttar Pradesh 1,46,811 (14.24%, stated); Maharashtra 1,46,810 (about 14.24%, computed; the one-unit gap with UP may be a typo); Rajasthan 1,30,540 (about 12.66%, computed); Madhya Pradesh 1,20,956 (11.73%, stated); Gujarat 92,529 (about 8.97%, computed). Top five = 6,37,646 = about 61.9% (computed). [Single source] — [Tractor Gyan FY26, S1](https://tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/2368/retail-tractor-sales-in-fy-2026-and-march-2026)
- Rank sensitivity: Tractor Gyan's year-to-date table through February 2026 put Maharashtra first with 1,40,287 units (14.76%). [Single source, via search summary] — [Tractor Gyan Feb 2026](https://tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/2309/retail-tractor-sales-in-february-2026)
- FY25 (Apr 2024 to Mar 2025): national 8,69,874; UP 1,39,886 (about 16.1% of national, computed). [Single source] — [S2](https://tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/1854/top-20-districts-with-highest-tractor-registrations-fy-2025)
- Region shares FY26: North "over a third" of sales with moderating growth; West strongest at "nearly 40%" with growth up to 28%; East has the highest commercial-use share (Bihar, West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha); no South figure. [Single source, citing CRISIL] — [Autocar Pro, 10 Apr 2026, S15](https://www.autocarpro.in/analysis/the-million-unit-milestone-fy26-redefines-indias-tractor-market-132056)
- National HP mix: 41-50 HP rose from under half of sales in FY19 to nearly two-thirds in FY26, at the expense of 31-40 HP; 51 HP and above is "a small share". [Single source] — [S15]
- State HP mix (dated): in FY18 Punjab sold 20,947 tractors, 42% above 50 HP versus 8% nationally; Haryana 20%; UP 8%; Punjab stock "more than 5.20 lakh". [Single source, 8 years old] — [Tribune, 16 Jun 2018, S16](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/business/affluent-farmers-drive-sale-of-high-end-tractors-in-punjab-606280)
- Recent HP signal: Punjab was the top state for above-50 HP (TREM IV) retail in April-May 2025 with 565 units, 20.21% of that segment. [Single source] — [S29](https://tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/1860/above-50hp-trem-iv-tractor-sales-may-2025)
- South: paddy drives roughly 35-40% of southern tractor volume; Escorts' Powertrac has up to 15% share in northern strongholds but about 3% in the South; shift from sub-40 HP to 45-50 HP after the GST cut. [Single source] — [Autocar Pro, 4 Mar 2026, S17](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/escorts-kubota-targets-doubling-south-india-market-share-to-tap-paddy-growth-131472)
- Custom hiring centres (2025): Punjab 11,148 (highest), then Andhra Pradesh and Haryana; 75,915 nationally. [Single source] — [S15]
- FY26 demand and cash drivers cited: above-normal monsoon; PM-KISAN about Rs 78,000 crore in four instalments; Maharashtra Rs 6,000 top-up; Bihar Rs 3,000 top-up plus 40-80% machinery subsidy; about 95% of tractors bought on credit; replacement of 2018-19 purchases. [Single source] — [S15]

### Inferences
- [Interpretation] Unit volume sits in UP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, MP and Gujarat; per-tractor value and large rear tyres on 50+ HP skew to Punjab and Haryana.
- [Interpretation] With about two-thirds of sales now 41-50 HP, tyre sizes may be more uniform nationally than the old north-versus-rest split implies; no tyre-size data was found to confirm.

### Gaps
- FY26 full-year totals for Punjab, Haryana, Karnataka, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana: Data unavailable – requires primary research (VAHAN dashboard query). A snippet giving Karnataka 14,672 and Bihar 9,357 sits in an article titled Q1 FY26, so its period is ambiguous; not used.
- State-wise tractor stock newer than IFPRI 2012: not found (only Punjab ">5.20 lakh" in 2018).
- HP mix by state for FY26: Data unavailable – requires primary research.

## 7. Crop calendar and cash-flow timing

### Takeaway
Sourcing is weak: only wheat and paddy procurement timing and the UP cane payment rule were found in news sources. Wheat procurement runs April to late May in the north (from mid-March in MP and Gujarat); paddy procurement starts 27 Sep to 1 Oct in Haryana and Punjab; UP cane is payable within 14 days but arrears recur.

### Cited Findings
- Wheat: procurement begins mid-March in MP and Gujarat and 1 April in Rajasthan, Haryana, UP and Punjab; Punjab and Haryana typically close mid-to-late May (extended to 31 May in 2022). 2025-26 central-pool wheat: Punjab 106.83 lakh tonnes, MP 69.24, Haryana 67.45; UP largely private purchase. Payment goes to farmers' bank accounts. [Single source, news summaries] — [Rural Voice](https://eng.ruralvoice.in/wheat-procurement-reaches-263-lakh-tonnes-up-emerges-as-private-procurement-hub-due-to-lower-prices), [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/government-wheat-procurement-reaches-6-mn-tonne-so-far-in-2025-26-season-3494732)
- Paddy: 2024 procurement began 1 Oct in Punjab and 27 Sep in Haryana; by 29 Oct 2024 about Rs 13,211 crore had been released to 3.5 lakh Punjab farmers and about Rs 10,529 crore in Haryana. [Single source] — [Newsonair, 30 Oct 2024, S25](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/paddy-procurement-in-full-swing-in-punjab-over-13000-crore-released-to-farmers)
- UP sugarcane: mills must pay within 14 days of supply or 15% annual interest (Sugarcane Control Order 1966). In 2025-26 Pilibhit farmers were owed over Rs 220 crore about 50 days into the season and Sambhal mills still owed money in April 2026. [Single source] — [Chini Mandi, S19](https://www.chinimandi.com/?p=357634)
- Generic calendar (low-reliability exam-prep and agri sites): kharif sowing about June-July, harvest about September-October; rabi sowing about October-December, harvest March-May; sugarcane 10-18 months. State-specific windows for the focus states and cotton timing not found. [Unverified secondary claim] — [Kshema](https://kshema.co/kharif-and-rabi-crops-differences)
- Bathinda (2011 plan): cotton and rice in kharif, wheat in rabi, cropping intensity 187% [S11].

### Inferences
- [Interpretation] The two big cash windows for Punjab and Haryana are April-May (wheat) and October-November (paddy). For UP, cane payments are due within 14 days of supply, but actual cash timing is irregular where arrears exist.
