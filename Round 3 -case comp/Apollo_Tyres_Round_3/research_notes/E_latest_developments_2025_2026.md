## 3. Policy and regulation

### Takeaway
The 3 Sept 2025 GST decision (effective 22 Sept 2025) cut tractors from 12% to 5% and tractor tyres/tubes/parts from 18% to 5%, a double tailwind for farm-tyre OE and replacement. TREM V for tractors starts 1 Oct 2026 only for under-25 hp and over-75 hp, with the 25-75 hp mainstream band deferred to 2028-2032 (draft; final status unconfirmed). Trade remedies and raw-material duty relief are mostly about inputs, not finished tyres.

### Cited Findings
- **GST (56th GST Council, announced 3 Sept 2025; effective 22 Sept 2025) [Verified fact]**: tractors 12% -> 5%; tractor tyres, tubes and parts 18% -> 5%; road tractors for semi-trailers above 1800 cc 28% -> 18%; other new pneumatic tyres (cars, trucks, two-wheelers) 28% -> 18%. Reported effect: tractor ex-showroom prices down Rs 40,000-60,000 per unit [Single source]; CRISIL calls the GST cut the main catalyst of FY26 tractor volume growth (about +22%). CEAT CEO Arnab Banerjee (13 Sept 2025): "Tractor sales should also see a healthy lift"; farm tyres ~10% of CEAT revenue. — [Autocar Pro, 3 Sep 2025](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/gst-on-tractors-and-tractor-parts-cut-to-5-128423) (rates; its text gives no effective date); [Apollo release](https://corporate.apollotyres.com/press-and-media/news/corporate/gst-benefit) and [CRISIL 18 Mar 2026](https://www.crisilratings.com/en/home/newsroom/press-releases/2026/03/high-base-effect-el-nino-dampener-for-tractor-sales-growth-next-fiscal.html) (effective 22 Sept 2025); [Republic/CEAT, 13 Sep 2025](https://www.republicworld.com/business/ceat-sees-tax-cuts-driving-demand-for-tractor-and-entry-level-motorcycle-tyres). A Busy.in page still shows stale rates; ignore. Why it matters: lowers cost of owning and replacing tyres on a tractor; makes September 2025 a high comparison base for FY27 volumes.
- **TREM V (tractor emission norms) [Single source for draft; final notification unconfirmed]**: MoRTH draft (reported 3 Mar 2026): below 25 hp and above 75 hp move to TREM V from 1 Oct 2026; 25-50 hp to TREM IIIAA from 1 Apr 2028 then TREM V 1 Apr 2032; 50-75 hp (currently TREM IV) to TREM V in 2032. CRISIL (18 Mar 2026) says the 25-75 hp band is ~90% of volume and would be unaffected until FY2032; under the earlier all-segment 1 Apr 2026 plan prices in that band could have risen 15-20%. A July 2026 Tribune item (seen via snippet) says norms take effect in phases from 1 Oct 2026; whether the final gazette kept the 25-75 hp deferral was not confirmed. Pull-forward buying ahead of TREM V is cited as a FY26 volume driver. — [Autocar Pro, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/govt-drafts-trem-v-for-tractors-from-october-2026-25%e2%80%9375-hp-segment-gets-a-breather-131469); [CRISIL](https://www.crisilratings.com/en/home/newsroom/press-releases/2026/03/high-base-effect-el-nino-dampener-for-tractor-sales-growth-next-fiscal.html). Why it matters: the mainstream farmer tractor stays cheap and unchanged for years, so no emission-driven price shock is expected in the core segment.
- **Import restrictions / trade remedies [Single source]**: June 2026 anti-dumping duty (5 years, USD 75-1,748 per tonne, notification dated 19 June 2026) on sulphenamide accelerators from China, EU, US, a tyre-chemical input; DGTR probe on a synthetic rubber used for tyre inner liners from US and China started Nov 2025; temporary customs-duty exemption on synthetic rubber and some resins to 30 June 2026. A finished-tyre duty on Chinese tyres: an Apollo Q1 FY27 call summary says anti-dumping duties on Chinese tyres are expected to help lower-end brands, but no 2026 duty notification on finished tyres was found; earlier reports say India decided against extending the older China tyre duties (date unclear). — [AngelOne](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/india-enforces-anti-dumping-duty-on-chemical-imports-from-china-eu-us); [Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/dgtr-initiates-dumping-probe-against-us-and-china-over-key-tyre-rubber-129607); [Autocar Pro on ATMA, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tyre-industry-body-seeks-duty-relief-amid-west-asia-supply-disruptions-132307).

## 4. Industry backdrop (tractors, monsoon, MSP, rubber, prices)

### Takeaway
FY26 was a record tractor year (about 11.6 lakh domestic wholesale, +23.5%) on the GST cut and a good 2025 monsoon (108% of LPA). FY27 year-to-date started very strong (April +27%, April-July +19%) but turned negative at company level in Sept-Oct 2026 on a high base and a shifted festive calendar, while the 2026 monsoon ended 13% below normal and El Nino peaks in November. Natural rubber hit a record Rs 284.50/kg on 13 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **FY26 domestic tractors [Verified fact on wholesale; retail single source]**: TMA domestic dispatches 11,60,231 units, +23.47% (implied FY25 ~9.4 lakh, my back-calculation; FY25 absolute not retrieved); exports 1,05,593 (+7%). Retail (FADA) 10,50,077 vs 8,82,825 in FY25 (+18.9%), crossing 10 lakh for the first time; rural 81% of retail, urban growing faster (22.4% vs 18.2%). March 2026 domestic wholesale 1,03,193 (+29%, flattered by Navratri timing). Drivers: GST cut, above-normal monsoon, MSP hikes, state subsidies, TREM V pull-forward, ~95% of purchases on credit with 125 bp of rate cuts, 2018-19 replacement cycle. — [Autocar Pro, 10 Apr 2026 (TMA)](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/tractor-industry-ends-fy26-on-a-high-march-domestic-sales-rise-29-on-festive-demand-132068); [Autocar Pro analysis, 10 Apr 2026](https://www.autocarpro.in/analysis/the-million-unit-milestone-fy26-redefines-indias-tractor-market-132056); [TractorGyan](https://www.tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/2337/indias-tractor-industry-crosses-1m-unit-sales-in-fy-2026-set-for-record-year); CRISIL (above) estimates ~+22%.
