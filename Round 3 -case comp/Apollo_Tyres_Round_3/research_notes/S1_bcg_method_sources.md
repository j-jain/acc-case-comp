**Rule for the BCG lens.** Anything the re-analysis attributes to BCG must come from a page listed in this note or in S2/S2b, fetched and read by us, and may be quoted or summarised only as far as that page goes. Every page in this note was fetched on 7 November 2026. Text in double quotes is verbatim as printed (including any dashes printed inside it); text outside quote marks is our summary. Descriptions of BCG by coaching sites, forums, Wikipedia or press are labelled [Secondary on BCG] and are never written as BCG's words. BCG has no part in this work and is never to be described as advising Apollo. "Undated" means the page prints no author or date; its only date is a footer "© 2026 Boston Consulting Group".

Evidence labels: [Verified fact], [Fact - single source], [Reported by press], [BCG-published], [Secondary on BCG], [Interpretation]. Where nothing reliable exists a row says exactly: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
