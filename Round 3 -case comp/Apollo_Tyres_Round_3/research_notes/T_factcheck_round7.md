# T. Fact-check, round 7: the BCG-lens files against their primary sources (7 Nov 2026)

**Method.** Each of the 36 claim groups was traced from its wording in `BCG_Lens\C02` to `C08` to the URL in the S1 to S7 notes (Q6 for the Goodyear warranty) and the source was read directly on 7 November 2026. Thirty-one bcg.com, dice.bcg.com and third-party pages were read with the fetch tool, which returns verbatim sentences; four long pages needed a second read at an offset. Twenty-two PDFs were obtained: the Apollo annual report, four decks, three call transcripts, the AERC sugarcane study, the PIB annexure, the Tolins RHP, the Hero release and two media-publications.bcg.com PDFs by curl into a fresh scratch directory; the seven web-assets.bcg.com PDFs refused curl ("Access Denied") and were taken from the fetch tool's saved copy instead. All PDFs were converted with pdftotext and searched; the PIB table and the RHP's Figure 20 were read as page images because they are graphics. Prices and dividends came from the Yahoo Finance chart feed (unadjusted NSE closes). One web search was used, to find the Hero release. Nothing else was blocked. Quotations keep the source's own spelling and errors; the source's dashes appear as em-dashes inside quotation marks only.
