## 5. How the BCG measurement pages map onto a 30-district four-arm test

[Interpretation; brief.] The test in `M08_Delivery.md` (30 districts; four tehsils a district drawn by lot, one per arm, about 29 tehsils an arm; primary metric Apollo's farm replacement billing in the tehsil against 24 months of history; a 1,160-owner first-mention survey per arm; gates of 2, 5 and 3 share points for the offer, the field engine and the cricket layer) matches BCG's published method on five points and departs on five.

Matches. (1) Units: "markets/geos, not individuals, as experimental units"; the tehsil is the unit. (2) Definition: "randomly applying a marketing activity to one (test) group while withholding it from the other (control) group" is Arm 0. (3) "Running power analysis to determine test budget and duration" is M08's minimum-detectable-effect table. (4) The four legs: "Modeling" is the billing baseline, "Incrementality Experiments" the arms, "Customer Insights" the owner survey, "Execution metrics" the dealer logs of registrations, stock and match days. (5) "Align KPIs to forge a common definition of success." is the gate table agreed with finance.

Departures for C11. (a) Matching: BCG's bank matched test and control to a "Pearson correlation of ≥95%" on sales; M08 draws by lot. Keep the draw, but first use the 24 months of billing to check that a district's four tehsils track each other, and swap a district whose tehsils do not. (b) The prior: rigorous testers "typically find that 20% to 40% of their active programs deliver marginal to negative lift"; state that as the cricket arm's prior, and describe Arm 0 as "an investment in information, not a cost to revenue." (c) Brand lift: no BCG page gives a survey method; the first-mention survey rests on the misattribution evidence in `Q7` section 3, not on BCG. (d) Horizon: BCG says capability takes "two to three years" and one OEM "12 to16 months to show a smooth trend"; the single season to January 2028 can read sales but will not give Apollo a settled measurement system by the March 2028 renewal. (e) Registration as a metric: BCG's "known and contactable" range of 1% to more than 60% is C06's yardstick. Report, per arm, the share of farm-tyre buyers registered at purchase, with the dealer doing the registering (the GoodLife model), and set C06's target inside BCG's range rather than at a figure no Indian tyre maker has published. The 76ers case then reads correctly: a registered base is what a rights holder sells to the next sponsor; for Apollo it is what the company carries into the 2028 renewal to price the asset on known farm buyers rather than on reach.

4. The 76ers case is a team's result: "fan participation" is undefined and the sponsorship sale is the rights holder's revenue. Not sponsorship lift. Likewise the 1% to 60% range is a team's fan conversion, not a product-registration rate; used in section 5 as a yardstick only.
5. "Beyond Media Rights": the web fetch truncated at 100,000 characters; the full PDF confirms nothing is addressed to sponsors and adds the Fantasy Premier League figures the web pass missed. PDF metadata says 18 March 2026; the article says 5 February 2026.
6. The esports 45% was in the brief, not in the page read; recorded as unconfirmed.
7. BCG X Media Optimization figures are client claims without base or method; only the stated position is carried into C11.
8. Apollo's "call Apollo Direct within a month" wording survives only in search snippets of a page that returned 404; the live page confirms a warranty number and a toll-free line but not the time limit or data fields. Labelled [Reported].
9. Mahindra's "2M+ Farmers Impacted" is not a registered-user count (its footnote folds in content viewers); only downloads and MAU, as of 30 June 2022, are used.
10. TAFE's count was 80,000 in February 2019 and 85,000 on an undated 2026 page; cited with both dates. ICC and BCCI app figures are third-party install estimates on differing dates; not compared with each other.
11. Hero GoodLife 45 million (April 2023) against 10 million (June 2012): two company statements at different dates, not a contradiction; no registration share can be computed from the release.
12. The Cricket Australia item is a press relay of advisory work, not a BCG publication, and is not used as BCG's view of cricket.
