**Reading for C08** [Interpretation]: on the patent scale our 30-district test scores D = 3 (21 January to the August 2027 gate is about seven months, "4-8 months"). With I, C1, C2 and E all at 2 the score is 3 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 2 = 15, "Touch and go" on BCG's 2021 bands; a quarterly learning milestone (D = 2) gives 14, the top of "Set up for success". A part-time team pushes E and I towards 3; each step on I or C1 costs two points.

## 5. Cautions and discrepancies

1. **Zone cut-offs differ.** Patent: under 14 likely to succeed, 14 to 21 hard to predict, over 21 "Woe Zone". BCG 2021 exhibit: 7 to 14 "Set up for success", 14 to 17 "Touch and go", 17 to 28 "In danger of failing". Secondary write-ups of the HBR article give 7/14/17/28, but the HBR body was unreadable. For C08 use the 2021 BCG exhibit and footnote the patent.
2. **Formula.** dice.bcg.com and the patent description agree on D + 2I + 2C1 + C2 + E. The patent claims as rendered on Google Patents show variant strings ("D+2I+2C1C2+E", "D+2I−2C1+C2+E"); treat as rendering faults.
3. **Research base** is stated three ways (row 1.12). Quote one source, not a blend.
4. **Calculator granularity.** Seven steps for I, C1, C2 on the live calculator against four in the patent; any number we print is our mapping of label to patent score.
5. **Failure rates are not one number.** "Two out of three" (2005), "at least half" (2012), "50%" to "75%" (2017), "Some 75 percent" (2014, 2003 to 2013 cohort), "over 70%" of "more than 3,000" (2021), "70% of digital transformations" (2020), "Three out of four" (2026 page). Cite one with date and base; 2021 has the largest stated base.
6. **Smart Simplicity figures measure different things.** "Sixfold" is the BCG Complexity Index (external); "35-fold" is procedures (internal), consistent with "6.7 percent a year" over 55 years (1.067^55 is about 35). The 2016 "between 50% and 350%" covers 15 years and a wider list. The report-writing (over 40%) and meeting (30 to 60%) shares apply only to "the top 20 percent of complicated organizations".
7. **Rule wording varies** ("what your people do" in 2014; "really do" in 2016). Quote one source.
8. **Authorship.** Patent: Keenan, Conlon, Jackson. HBR: Sirkin, Keenan, Jackson.
9. **Marks.** BCG states DICE is its registered trademark; the booklet prints "Smart Simplicity®". Name them as BCG's tools; never imply BCG involvement with Apollo.
10. **AI-only pilot evidence.** Rows 3.10 and 3.11 concern AI; applying them to a dealer-settled tyre guarantee is interpretation.
11. **Dates.** Capability pages are undated, "© 2026", newest items August 2026. Nothing BCG-published on DICE itself is newer than the November 2021 report and the dice.bcg.com site.
