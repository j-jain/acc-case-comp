# C11. Sources and the fact-check

Prepared 7 November 2026. Part of the BCG-lens re-analysis of the Apollo Round 3 case.

**What this file is.** The register of every BCG page and PDF the BCG lens relies on, with its date and what was taken from it; a map of the other sources by research note; the round-7 fact-check of the C files; and the audits run on the folder. The McKinsey-lens register is `..\17_Source_Register_and_Verification.md` sections 11 to 14 and the Bain-lens register is `..\Bain_Lens\B11_Sources_and_Factcheck.md`; neither is repeated.

**Rules applied.**

1. Anything attributed to BCG comes from a bcg.com page (including careers.bcg.com and dice.bcg.com), a BCG PDF on web-assets.bcg.com or media-publications.bcg.com, or BCG's own patent, fetched and read on 7 November 2026; quotes are verbatim; dates are as printed; undated pages are cited "Undated, fetched 7 Nov 2026".
2. Nothing in this folder says or implies that BCG advised Apollo or wrote any of this. Every C file carries the sentence "It is not BCG's work."
3. Where a first reading of a page was wrong, the correction is recorded (section 1.6) and applied.
4. Descriptions of BCG's method by coaching sites, forums or press are labelled [Secondary on BCG] and never attributed to BCG (`..\research_notes\S1_bcg_method_sources.md` section 5).
5. Where BCG published on tyres, the files say precisely what it covers (global, listed makers, shareholder returns) and that it does not cover farm, OE, dealers or India by name.
