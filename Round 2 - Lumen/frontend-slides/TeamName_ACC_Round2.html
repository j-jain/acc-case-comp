Tradition
The Four Letters
A shared past
  • Every Lumenite writes four letters in a life, each at home with the household gathered, on the day they reach that age.
  • Letters 1–3 stay sealed. At 250 you open them, read your life back, and write the fourth.
  • The 250 letter is for your children and theirs, kept with your photo as the family’s record of who you were.
  • After death, the letters go to the Lumen Archive, open to any future Lumenite if the writer allowed it while alive.
  • In Year 1, the ~8,300 Lumenites already past 250 write first, so history starts on day one.

Belonging: every family gains a written past, and the Archive grows into the whole planet’s shared history.

Only on Lumen: a 300-year life lets you open your own letters 225 years after the first.

Festival
The Carrying
A shared place · Day 1 of the 13th moon
  • Each of the 100 districts carries one flame to every home it has: ~125 homes, door to door from first light.
  • Every household adds what fuel it can. Fuel is precious gas, so each home gives part of its wealth.
  • At dusk the flame is set in the district square for all 500 to see. It burns through the night for as long as the district’s gift lasts.

Belonging: the flame exists only because every door gave. Fire stands for hope and togetherness.

Only on Lumen: the fuel is the currency itself, and a 15-hour night gives the flame its stage.

Entertainment
The Hidden Head League
A shared rhythm · Every week
  • Each season, every district draws 20 champions by lot; one is secretly the leader.
  • Each week the districts play in pairs (50 matches).
  • Each round, the hidden leader quietly steers their side through a task; the other side watches who the players follow and names one suspect. A wrong name clears that suspect and costs the next guess; the first side to name the other’s leader wins.
  • Moons 1–12 are the league; in the 13th moon the top 16 play a knockout, with the final on the last night of the year.
  • Spectators bet in gas on the leader: the earlier the bet, the bigger the payout (up to 20× in round 1, falling as suspects are cleared). Stakes are capped; the house cut pays the champion district’s prize.

Belonging: a weekly reason to leave work and stand with your district. Every adult plays about 12 seasons in a life.

Only on Lumen: the planet’s real leader is secret, so its favourite game is finding one.
