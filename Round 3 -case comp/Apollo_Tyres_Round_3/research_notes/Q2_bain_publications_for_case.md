## 5. Discrepancies corrected before use

Eighteen points where a first reading of these pages was wrong; all corrected in the Bain lens:

1. The Bain Academy brochure names "Answer First" but does not define it; it is a 2020 draft export.
2. "More than 80% of major change efforts fall short" is on What We Believe, not on the Results Delivery page.
3. The Colorado College deck is a 2015 recruiting talk, with "typical week" slides recycled from 2009.
4. Bain's case-interview page never says "answer first", "hypothesis", "80/20" or "so what".
5. "Route to a brand's best strategy" does not say share explains half of profitability; it says share alone does not correlate with profitability, and premium products earn more with smaller share.
6. The "about 5% of weak followers" finding is on the Beyond the Core excerpt; "more than three in four" adjacency failures is in Lin and Zook, 29 March 2004.
7. The two hidden-asset pages name the three types differently.
8. "Earned Growth Rate" and its formula are not on Bain's Net Promoter 3.0 page; cite HBR November-December 2021.
9. The "10 to 20 points" of NPS in the episodes article is about digital-channel users, not about episode redesign.
10. The private-equity page has no "first 100 days"; the route-to-market page never uses "dealer".
11. The Zero-Click playbook has three steps and a three-tier scorecard, not "four rules"; the wealth-manager figure is "exceeded $50 million" of incremental net new assets.
12. The 54% Latino figure is in the press release, not the infographic; it is a stated likelihood.
13. The company is "Tata Steel Wire Division (TSWD)".
14. The three NPS layers are strategic, relationship, and operational-and-customer-journey.
15. "Winning with India's Shoppers" refers to "the leading hair oil brand"; the sample is "nearly 80,000".
16. The "more than 1 lakh" is PACS plus FPOs combined; ITC's figure is about 6,100 groups in about 11,000 villages.
17. Neither aftermarket article gives an uplift or profit-share percentage.
18. HBR May-June 1998 carries both "Profit Pools: A Fresh Look at Strategy" and "How to Map Your Industry's Profit Pool"; the Bain lens cites the latter by its title.
