# V. Fact-check, round 8: the replacement-decision files against their sources (8 Nov 2026)

**Method.** On 8 November 2026 each claim was found in `..\Replacement_Decision\RD00` to `RD03`, its URL taken from U1 to U4, and the source opened. Seven PDFs (J.D. Power Japan 2015, DAESI, Yaswanth et al., Niraj Kumar, Bridgestone 2023, Apollo's Q3 FY26 and Q1 FY27 transcripts) were downloaded to an empty folder and read as text. Pages refused by the fetch tool were downloaded and searched; the J.D. Power 2018 release, the YouTube comments and the court records were read in the in-app browser (no login, no consent, nothing posted), YouTube through the page's own comment requests, matching comment IDs. Six of 15 permitted searches were used (calendar; J.D. Power 2026). The corpus was recounted with a read-only script. No analysis or research file was edited. Quotes are kept short.
