## 3. What is said about Bain by others (not attributable to Bain)

| Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Answer First means stating a defensible hypothesis from limited data, building an issue structure that only tests it, then looking for "evidence that would break your hypothesis" | strategycase.com (a coaching site) on Bain's case format | Secondary; consistent with the brochure's terms |
| McKinsey's equivalent label is a "Day 1 answer" | A coach on preplounge.com | Secondary |
| 80/20 at Bain means "80% of the best answer, provided this could be implemented by the client"; a check against over-analysis | A Bain intern's blog (University of Richmond, 2019); a Bain MBA hire quoted by Poets and Quants (2023) | Secondary; former or current staff, not a Bain page |
| The "Monday-morning test": every recommendation must be something the client can act on Monday morning; "the burden of proof is on us" | A former Bain consultant (firmsconsulting.com) | Secondary |
| A Bain answer "forms the view first", where a McKinsey answer opens with a broad structure; Bain interviews are candidate-led, McKinsey's interviewer-led; Bain is "results-focused, implementation emphasis" | strategycase.com; casestar.io; preplounge.com | Reputational; several coaches say firm-level differences are small and office-level ones larger |
| Bain is "the leader in private equity" consulting | A former principal on preplounge.com; Bain's own PE page claims 6,500+ portfolio cases | Bain's page supports the scale, not the ranking |

## 4. What was looked for and not found on Bain's pages

- A Bain definition of "Answer First" (only the course title).
- Any Bain page using "so what", "blueprint", "mini-case" or "ghost deck" as terms of art.
- A Bain-prescribed deck template ("answer, three reasons, actions" is a coaching-site description).
- A Bain page on relative market share or the experience curve as such (the experience curve is BCG's; Bain's "leadership economics" is the nearest).
- Any Bain page on tyres, tractors, farm machinery, cricket, the IPL or the BCCI.

For each: not attributable to Bain; used, where at all, as "consulting practice" with the secondary source named.
