# Lumen — verified case facts, derived numbers, assumption log

Source: `Round 2 Mini Case - Lumen.pdf` (2 pages, copied into this folder from Downloads). Every line below was re-read against the PDF on 29 Oct 2026. Nothing from Round 1 (Meridian Racing) is used.

## 1. The brief, as written

**Problem statement.** Lumen is a small planet orbiting the Sun. Its people were born there and have never known any other world. Food, shelter and work are all taken care of, and life runs smoothly. What Lumen doesn't have is a culture: no festivals, no traditions, nothing people look forward to. Lumenites understand what culture is; they just haven't created any of their own. It's starting to show: people finish work and go home alone, community gatherings are close to empty, and most residents describe life as "functional but empty". The Lumen Council has asked us to design the planet's culture from scratch.

**About Lumen and Lumenites (all 11 facts, verbatim in substance).**

| # | Fact |
|---|---|
| 1 | Orbit, year and day: Lumen orbits the Sun; one Lumen year is **520 Earth days**; one day lasts **30 Earth hours**, with **15 hours of light and 15 hours of dark**. |
| 2 | Moon: Lumen has **one moon**. |
| 3 | Population: **50,000 people, in a single concentrated area**. The largest gathering space holds **1,000 people** at a time. |
| 4 | Currency: economy not dissimilar to a capitalist economy on Earth, but instead of precious metals they trade in **precious gases**. |
| 5 | Government: reporting to a **secret Head of Government**. |
| 6 | Natural elements: same as Earth, i.e. **Water, Air, Fire, Sky, Earth**. |
| 7 | Work: **all adult Lumenites work**, and they choose to occupy their time with work **due to the absence of meaningful/cultural shared experiences**. |
| 8 | Environment: **astronomical events and other natural phenomena are common sightings**. |
| 9 | Biology: Lumenites sleep in **at least one 10-hour stretch**. |
| 10 | No past: Lumen has **no history, no religion and no inherited traditions**. Earth and its culture don't exist for them, so **ideas copied from Earth's festivals won't count**. |
| 11 | Age: life expectancy is **300 years**; an adult could reasonably work till **250**; maturity (equivalent of 18 on Earth) is attained at **50**. |

## 3. Things the case does NOT say (each becomes a stated assumption)

| Gap | Working assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Are ages in Lumen years or Earth years? | **Lumen years** (the planet's own unit). | Either way lives are very long; 50-year cohorts are the natural generational beat. |
| Seasons? | The constant 15/15 split and no mention of seasons → **no axial tilt, no seasons, no harvest**. | Earth's calendar is built on seasons and harvests; Lumen's can't be. Time must be marked by the moon, the sky and counting. |
| Moon's cycle length | Not given → **we may define it** (e.g. 32 days gives 13 "moons" a year). | Lets the calendar have a lunar beat without contradicting the case. |
| Sleep timing | "At least one 10-hour stretch" → **one main sleep, flexible timing**; people may sleep in daylight. | A planet where the night is 15 hours and people are awake for ≥5 of them has a built-in "dark hours" slot. |
| What are "precious gases"? | **Noble gases** (helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon): rare, inert, and they **glow in distinct colours when charged**. | "Lumen" is the unit of light. Glowing currency is the most Lumen-native material in the case. (Colours to be verified.) |
| Venue cap | Only the largest **built** space is 1,000; **open air is uncapped**; homes exist. | Festivals can be sky-based, street-based, home-based or relayed through the 1,000 space. |
| Who is the Council? | The client; **plural**, public. The Head of Government is **secret even from us**. | Any design that depends on the Head appearing is fragile. |
| What counts as "copied from Earth"? | A judge in India will instantly recognise Diwali, Holi, Christmas, Ramadan, Onam, Durga Puja in form or name. **Mechanisms may be borrowed; forms, names and props may not.** | This is the biggest grading risk. |
| Children | Maturity at 50 → **people are "young" for 50 Lumen years**; nothing is said about family structure or birth rate. | Life-stage rituals have an unusually long childhood to work with. |
| Technology level | "Life runs smoothly", astronomical events are "sightings" → **modern, comfortable, not hyper-advanced**; they look up with their eyes. | Keeps designs grounded (no holograms needed). |
| Weather | Natural phenomena are common → **some weather and sky drama exists** even without seasons. | Unscheduled events are a usable trigger. |

## 4. Constraints any answer must satisfy (checklist)

1. Exactly the three required components, clearly labelled: major festival · recurring tradition/ritual · form of entertainment.
2. Nothing that is an Earth festival in disguise (name, props, story), even if it uses the same mechanism.
3. Consistent with the 416-day year, the 30-hour day with a 15-hour night, the single moon, and the 50/200/50 life stages.
4. A participation path for all 50,000 when the biggest room holds 1,000.
5. Uses what the case deliberately planted: the secret Head, precious gases, the five elements, common astronomical events, the 10-hour sleep, the no-past, the over-work.
6. Answers the actual symptom: people go home alone, gatherings are empty, life is "functional but empty". The festival must be something people **look forward to**.

## 5. Planted hooks a judge will look for

| Hook in the case | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| "Lumen" | SI unit of luminous flux: the planet is named for light. |
| Precious gases | Noble gases glow; a currency you can light up. |
| 15 h light / 15 h dark + 10 h sleep | Guaranteed waking darkness every day; the night is a free venue. |
| One moon | The only natural clock besides the Sun; a lunar month is the obvious calendar unit. |
| 1,000-person cap vs 50,000 | Forces distributed, relayed, moving or home-based designs. |
| Secret Head of Government | Anonymity, masks, equality, intrigue; "anyone could be the Head". |
| Five elements incl. Sky | Water, Air, Fire, Sky, Earth is the classical five-element set; Sky is the odd one and the planet's most visible feature. |
| 300-year lives, maturity at 50 | Rare rituals (once in 50 years) are affordable; grudges and friendships last centuries. |
| No past | The first festival can be the invention of history itself. |
| Over-work | The culture must compete with work for time, or be built into it. |
