## Corrections needed

- **Claim 1 (GST):** Safe wording: "GST Council, 56th meeting, 3 Sep 2025: tractors 12% to 5%; tractor tyres and tubes 18% to 5%; effective 22 Sep 2025." Do not state one old rate for all "parts" (one outlet says 28% for HSN 8708 parts). Road tractors over 1800 cc are excluded (18%). PIB release not opened; secondary sources agree.
- **Claim 2:** Correct, but the wholesale number is described as "estimated", and FADA FY26 retail (10,50,077) is not the same series as Tractor Gyan's VAHAN-based national total (10,30,895, claim 3). Do not mix the two.
- **Claim 3:** Shares for Maharashtra (14.24%), Rajasthan (12.66%), Gujarat (8.98%) are not on the page; they are calculations. UP leads Maharashtra by one unit (1,46,811 vs 1,46,810), so say "joint top".
- **Claim 5:** 5.81 million is cumulative registrations (MoRTH), not tractors in use; "about 6 million four-wheel tractors" is a crude estimate from CSAM/UNESCAP 2016. The paper's "medium" is 2-10 ha and "small and marginal" is under 2 ha. Punjab + Haryana = 17.8% together.
- **Claim 6:** The 2014 study is titled the "2015" inaugural study; fieldwork July-Nov 2014, owners who bought a new tractor Jul 2012-Nov 2013. The 6.5 per 100 refers to excessive tyre wear. 2017 data seen only via Tyrepress.
- **Claim 7:** Source is MRF's own press page; inaugural study, so no trend can be claimed.
- **Claim 8:** The 49% and 20% sentences are not explicitly labelled "kharif" on the page; the 23%, 3.1%, 1.3%, 0.3% are.
- **Claim 9:** Studies are old and small: Sirsa = 2011 thesis (80 owners, two tehsils); Ahmednagar = 2003 thesis (about 60 respondents, 14 villages; transport 30.72%); Mahakoshal = Vegetos 2017 (survey year and sample not stated). Label as older studies; do not generalise.
- **Claim 11:** Use "12.6% below the long-period average (87% of LPA)" and "107.9% of LPA (about 108%)". IMD primary bulletins not opened.
- **Claim 12:** Portal listings, not dealer prices; the two portals differ by Rs 160-400 per tyre.
- **Claim 14:** 612 million is the cricket audience; 678 million is the whole sports audience (page headline). "Audience" = watched sports on TV/OTT for 30+ min in the past year. Free Dish 49 million = households, from the FY2024-25 annual report reported on 3 Apr 2026.
- **Claim 15:** Semi-medium is **9.55%** (not 9.43%) and medium **3.80%** (not 3.76%), computed from the official PIB count table (1,46,454 thousand holdings: 1,00,251 / 25,809 / 13,993 / 5,561 / 838). Marginal 68.45%, small 17.62%, large 0.57% are right. Official operated-area shares were NOT found; do not quote 24.03/23.54/23.61/19.96/8.85 (secondary, unverified). Average 1.08 ha: supported by search results citing PIB, not opened by me.
- **Claim 16:** COULD NOT CHECK in a document I could open; supported only by a search summary of a Lok Sabha annexure. Verify before use.
- **Claim 18:** Rs 34,000-75,000 is right and excludes 18% GST, but the PDF names no month or year: drop "September 2026" or confirm from the PAU Kisan Mela notice.
- **Claim 20:** McKinsey page not opened; the one-third-to-half figure is "industry research" cited by McKinsey. Re-check exact wording before putting it in quotation marks.
- **Claim 21:** Rewrite as: Castrol "over 1.5 lakh mechanics and retailers enrolled on Castrol Fast Scan (Sep 2019)"; Ambuja "over 70,000 contractors enrolled in Ambuja Abhimaan in 2021; 2.93 lakh by FY2025"; Pidilite "about 40,000 applicators in close to 300 Fevicol Champions Clubs (undated interview; an older annual report said 45,000+)".
- **Claim 24:** Cite Down To Earth (not ThePrint) for KCC 44.1%.
