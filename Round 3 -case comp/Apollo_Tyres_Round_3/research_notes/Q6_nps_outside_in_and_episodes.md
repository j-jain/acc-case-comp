# Q6. Owners and dealers outside-in: grievances, the claim episode, word of mouth (7 Nov 2026)

Round-6 research note for the Bain-lens re-analysis. Web research, 30 searches and about 75 page reads, 7 November 2026. Feeds `..\Bain_Lens\B04_Customer_NPS_Episodes_Value.md` and `B05`.

**What this is and is not.** Every count below is of items one researcher read and coded from public complaint, court and forum pages. It is not a survey. Complaint sites carry no praise, so the counts describe detractors only. The earlier YouTube coding (486 comments, `N3`) found tyre life and refused claims as the main themes; this note adds complaint boards, consumer-court judgments, warranty terms and the dealer side.

All rows [Verified fact: policy pages read] except BKT.

**Observed timings and inspectors, from consumer-court records** [Verified fact: judgments read]:

- MRF, Haryana State Commission, 14 November 2022: the dealer forwarded the claim on 5 June 2020; MRF's technical personnel inspected on 17 June; the report went to the tractor maker on 18 June; rejection on 6 August 2020, about two months; the cause coded "uneven tread wear caused due to unequal contact pressure"; the complaint dismissed.
- Apollo, Tamil Nadu State Commission, 23 February 2012 (a Sonalika on Apollo tyres): the dealer told the farmer "the Tyre Company will come and inspect and thereafter alone, the tyres could be exchanged"; Apollo's staff coded "Neglected Tread Cuts" and refused; a district award of Rs 1.5 lakh was reversed on appeal.
- Car claims: an MRF rejection SMS about two weeks after the dealer forwarded (Satara, November 2020); an Apollo rejection report eight days after deposit, with the engineer sending photographs on WhatsApp (June 2017).

**Net reading of the episode.** The dealer is the intake point; a company field engineer decides; the decision is contractually final; no maker publishes a settlement time; the commonest real-world cause of early rear-tyre wear (haulage with a trolley) is excluded in writing by one maker and was the ground of rejection in the MRF case.
