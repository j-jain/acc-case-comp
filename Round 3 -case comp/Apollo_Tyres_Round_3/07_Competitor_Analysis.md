# 07 Competitor Analysis: the Indian farm-tyre market and Apollo's rivals

Prepared 6 November 2026

**Purpose.** This file sets out who Apollo Tyres competes with for Indian farmers' tractor tyre purchases, how those rivals reach farmers, and what is and is not known about prices, warranties, dealers and shares. It is the evidence base for the competitive part of the case and proposes no strategy.

Full source list with reliability ratings: 17_Source_Register_and_Verification.md

> **How to read this file**
> - **[Verified fact]**: primary source (filing, regulator order, official release) or two independent credible sources.
> - **[Fact - single source]**: one credible source only. Likely, not confirmed.
> - **[Interpretation]**: our reading of the evidence.
> - **[Forecast]**: a projection or target by a company or analyst.
> - **[Unverified]**: a secondary claim (portal, blog, snippet) with no primary document behind it.
> - Any company statement about its own leadership or share is a **claim**, whatever its label. Gaps are never filled with estimates.

**Terms.** *OEM* (original equipment manufacturer) here means the tractor maker; OEM tyres are fitted on a new tractor at the factory. The *replacement market* is tyres bought later, when the originals wear out. *Bias tyres* have criss-crossed fabric layers and a stiff sidewall; they are India's standard, cheaper farm tyre. *Radial tyres* have cords running straight across, giving a flexible sidewall and wider footprint; they cost more and remain a niche in Indian farming.

Power studies, in 04_Farmer_Consumer_Behaviour.md; geography in 06_Rural_Distribution_and_Geography.md; diagnosis in 09_Problem_Diagnosis.md.

## 1. What matters most

- No neutral, current figure for the size of the Indian farm-tyre market or any company's share of it is public. Every share number in circulation is a company or broker claim. [Interpretation, after 37 searches found none]
- Goodyear India is the original-equipment supplier to six named tractor makers. Mahindra is Apollo's only confirmed tractor OEM customer. [Fact - single source each]
- MRF ranked No.1 in J.D. Power's 2018 India Tractor Tyre Satisfaction Index, with its biggest lead on durability. [Fact - single source: MRF release about a third-party study]
- On the common 13.6-28 rear size, portal prices for Apollo, Goodyear and MRF sit within about 6-8% of each other, Apollo lowest. [Interpretation of single-source portal prices]
- Apollo's farm warranty is 7 years on manufacturing defects. CEAT gives 7 years on tractor tyres made from January 2019 and MRF gives 6 years, each on the company's own policy page. Goodyear's 6 years is a portal claim. Warranty length does not separate the brands. [Verified fact for Apollo, MRF and CEAT; Unverified for Goodyear]
- CEAT (IPL partner to about 2028) and BKT (8 of 10 IPL teams in 2026) already hold IPL rights. Neither deal is stated to target farm tyres. [Verified fact]
- No rival has published sales or share results from a farmer-marketing programme. [Interpretation]
- Dealer margins, credit terms and dealers' influence on the choice are not measured in any public source. [Interpretation; the only margin figures are Unverified]

## 4. Competitor profiles

### Goodyear India

**Product positioning.** Vajra Super and Sampurna (rear); Ultra Grip launched 1 Jul 2026 in 14.9-28 only, sold on "deeper tread" and puncture and cut resistance. [Fact - single source] ([Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/goodyear-india-launches-ultra-grip-tractor-tire-for-farm-segment-133345))

**Distribution.** OE supplier to M&M, TAFE, Escorts, ITL (Sonalika), John Deere and Eicher (Dec 2022). [Fact - single source] ([HDFC Sec](https://www.hdfcsec.com/hsl.research.pdf/Goodyear%20-%20Initiating%20Coverage%20-%20141222.pdf)) FY26 plan: deepen OEM key accounts; "channel expansion" in replacement. [Fact - single source] ([MD&A](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/goodyear-india-ltd/management-discussions)) Dealer count: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

**Marketing to farmers and sponsorships.** Ultra Grip shown to about 100 dealers in Chandigarh. Farmer programmes and sponsorships: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

**Strengths and weaknesses.** Strength: OEM fitment. Weaknesses: farm dependence; "limited presence in the Commercial tyre category"; the US parent reviewed a sale of the India farm business from Apr 2025 (reported at Rs 2,500-2,700 crore) before keeping it on 5 Dec 2025. [Verified fact] ([Tyre Trends](https://www.tyre-trends.com/news/goodyear-shelves-plan-to-exit-india-farm-tyre-business-after-strategic-review))

### MRF

**Product positioning.** Shakti family and Krishi / Krishi Hi Lug, in all main rear and front sizes; claims high lug depth and wear-resistant compound. [Fact - single source] ([TractorGyan](https://tractorgyan.com/tractor-industry-news-blogs/1680/top-mrf-tractor-tyres-price-list-in-india)) Brand line "Kisan ka Dost". [Fact - single source]

**Distribution.** "Increasing share of business with tractor manufacturers" (FY26 MD&A) ([MD&A](https://www.indiainfoline.com/company/mrf-ltd/management-discussions)); farm the only OEM segment "unaffected" in Q1 FY26 ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/mrf-q1fy26-earnings-results-revenue-at-rs-7802-crore-up-7-percent-yoy)). [Fact - single source] Dealer count: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

**Marketing to farmers and sponsorships.** Publicises its J.D. Power No.1. Ended its 8-year ICC partnership in May 2024. [Verified fact] ([afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/mrf-ends-partnership-with-international-cricket-council-ahead-of-2024-t20-world-cup-report))

**Strengths and weaknesses.** Strengths: durability reputation; widest range; highest portal price, suggesting pricing power [Interpretation]. Weakness: no disclosed farm distribution or farmer programme.

### CEAT

**Product positioning.** Vardhan (c. 2020; "special deep lugs", "long tyre life"), Aayushmaan, Puddle X3; Farmax radials OE on Case IH and New Holland (2023, mainly export); acquiring Camso (Michelin's off-highway business) in Q2 FY26. [Fact - single source] ([TractorJunction](https://www.tractorjunction.com/tractor-news/ceat-specialty-launches-farm-tyre-range-vardhan/), [CEAT Q1 FY26 call](https://www.ceat.com/content/dam/ceat/ceat-revemp/pdf/ceat-earnings-call-transcript-q1fy26.pdf))

**Distribution.** About 50,000 outlets in 2022 (all categories), aiming for 1 lakh through kiranas and puncture shops in places of 5,000-10,000 people; the article concerned two-wheeler tyres. [Fact - single source] ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/ceat-aims-to-make-deeper-inroads-into-small-towns-eyes-1-lakh-outlets-in-2-3-years-coo-arnab-banerjee-news-217416)) 50% of sales through its exclusive channel. [Fact - single source] Farm count: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

**Marketing to farmers and sponsorships.** 15 tractor meets in UP, 2016. [Verified fact] IPL partner since 2015 (see section 8).

**Strengths and weaknesses.** Strengths: rural outlet model; radials. Weaknesses: farm about 10% of revenue; farm OEM growth mid-single-digit in Q1 FY26 [Fact - single source]; cricket aimed at cars.

### JK Tyre

**Product positioning.** Shresth / Shresth Plus and Sona. Shresth Plus launched at Krishi Darshan Expo, Hisar, Feb 2026; sizes, price and warranty not stated. "Premiumisation at the core" and higher-horsepower tractors. [Fact - single source] ([Equipment India](https://equipmentindia.com/construction-machinery-news/top-equipment-news/webexclusive/JK-Tyre-Strengthens-Farm-Portfolio-with-Launch-of-Shresth-Plus-/130460))

**Marketing to farmers and sponsorships.** Expo stall with live tractor stunts. Sponsorships not researched.

**Strengths and weaknesses.** Strength: rural retail format. Weaknesses: no farm-share data; Steel Wheels rollout numbers unknown.

**Strengths and weaknesses.** Strengths: agri scale; long rural sport portfolio. Weaknesses: small India base; cricket aimed at consumer tyres; attention split by on-highway entry.

[Fact - single source] ([Insolvency Tracker](https://insolvencytracker.in/2026/08/03/birla-tyres-targets-%E2%82%B93000-crore-revenue-as-himadri-revives-bankrupt-brand-with-premium-products-exports/))

## 8. Tyre brands already in cricket

- **CEAT.** IPL Strategic Timeout partner since 2015. A five-year "official partner" deal of about Rs 240 crore was announced 23 Feb 2024, running to about the 2028 season. Management ties cricket to "saliency" for four-wheeler tyres. [Verified fact] ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/quantum-brief/bcci-names-ceat-as-official-partner-for-ipl-to-sponsor-certain-segments-matches-24256.htm), [CEAT call](https://www.ceat.com/content/dam/ceat/ceat-revemp/pdf/ceat-earnings-call-transcript-q1fy26.pdf))

## 12. Implications for the case

- [Interpretation] Rivals reach the farmer through routes that do not depend on advertising: Goodyear through the tyres that arrive on the new tractor, MRF through a durability reputation, CEAT and JK through small-town outlets. Awareness alone will not dislodge them.
- [Interpretation] Nobody has published results from a farmer-marketing programme, so there is no outcome benchmark to borrow. Apollo would have to generate its own evidence, which makes measurement part of the strategy and not an add-on.
- [Interpretation] Prices for the common 13.6-28 rear tyre sit within about 6-8% of each other on the portals, with Apollo at the lower end. At near-parity prices the choice turns on trust in durability and on who recommends the tyre.
- [Interpretation] Cricket is crowded for tyre brands at IPL level (CEAT, BKT). The one cricket asset no rival can buy is the national team jersey, so Apollo's edge lies in what that asset uniquely allows, not in buying more cricket inventory.
- [Interpretation] Dealer margins, credit terms and the dealer's influence on the choice are unmeasured in public sources. A dealer and mechanic survey has to come before any incentive design.
- [Strategic hypothesis] Because most tractors leave the factory on a rival's tyres, a farmer's first-hand experience of tractor tyres is usually with Goodyear or MRF. Apollo has to create first-hand experience (trial, demonstration, a neighbour's tractor) and not only exposure.
