# RD03. What the replacement-decision research changes in the plan

Prepared 8 November 2026. Part of the replacement-decision analysis of the Apollo Round 3 case.

**What this file is.** Our own analysis. It says what `RD01_Who_Decides_the_Replacement.md` and `RD02_Cricket_for_the_Trade.md` change in the three re-analyses (`..\McKinsey_Lens\`, `..\Bain_Lens\`, `..\BCG_Lens\`) and in option D, the deck recommendation in `..\BCG_Lens\C10_BCG_vs_Bain_vs_McKinsey.md` section 4. It is not the work of any consulting firm.

**Labels.** [Computed], [Planning assumption], [Interpretation], [Strategic hypothesis]. Costs are planning assumptions; none is a forecast.

---

## 1. The answer in five lines

1. **The direction does not change.** All three lenses already rest on the owner's verdict on tyre life (the hours promise) and on paying the dealer and the mechanic per extra tyre. The research confirms both, and confirms that the jersey cannot sell the tyre on the screen. [Interpretation]
2. **The weight changes.** The trade closes about three in five replacements (RD01, analogue-based), so cricket rewards for the trade move from a small layer into the main use of the jersey in farm, tied to stock and registered tyres. [Interpretation]
3. **Three things are new:** a stocking reward in the December 2026 to March 2027 home window; a fast lane for factory-Apollo claims through the tractor dealer, because that claim is where the first replacement is often lost; and life numbers from registered owners as the message that travels between farmers. [Strategic hypothesis]
4. **One measurement comes first:** a counter intercept at 40 farm-tyre counters in the first buying season, which would be the first measurement anywhere of who chooses a tractor tyre at replacement (about Rs 0.1 crore). [Strategic hypothesis]
5. **The cost of option D barely moves:** about Rs 0.2 crore more to March 2028 if the slabs replace the Bain lens's top-300 hospitality, and about Rs 0.7 crore less if the village tournaments are also dropped. [Computed]
