**Doordarshan and the free-to-air rule.** Under the Sports Broadcasting Signals (Mandatory Sharing) Act 2007, as read by the Supreme Court on 22 August 2017, the shared feed of "events of national importance" may be carried only on Doordarshan's own terrestrial network and DD Free Dish, not on DD channels through cable or private DTH (indiantelevision.com, 28 September 2017). [Verified fact] Match-day pages state that India v New Zealand (January 2026), India v England (2026) and India's T20 World Cup 2026 games were on DD Sports for Free Dish and terrestrial users, with JioStar as rights holder. [Reported by press; the site is now unreachable] Whether the 2027 home Tests against Australia get DD carriage, and the size of the DD Free Dish household base: [Not found]. For a tractor owner's household without a paid DTH subscription, Free Dish is the likely route to an India match; this is the one reach question Apollo's owner survey must ask. [Interpretation]

## 3. Sales-lift evidence with a control

1. **Bain, "The Zero-Click Playbook: How Sports Sponsorships Build Preference Before Search"**, 9 June 2026, Milligan, Beaudin, Dennehy and Beale. [Verified fact: read at source] Bain's sentence: "Without a rigorous test-and-control design, sponsorship ROI is merely an opinion." Three-tier scorecard: brand impact, engagement, commercial outcomes. Cases as Bain states them:
   - A wealth manager invited high-net-worth clients to sports hospitality "while holding back a matched control group"; attendees' assets rose "meaningfully" against the control over several years; repeat attendees showed "significantly higher inflows and lower attrition"; "incremental net new assets attributable to the program exceeded $50 million" in one year.
   - P&G "Thank You, Mom": "$500 million in global incremental sales" (Bain dates it to the 2010 Olympics; award entries attribute $500 million global and +$200 million US to London 2012 and $130 million to Vancouver 2010; all self-reported). [Reported by press for the award entries]
   - Under Armour: a "95% increase in fourth-quarter footwear revenue" in 2016 on the Curry line (company-reported).
   - Roblox at Super Bowl LVII: 7.5 million visits in a week.
2. **Other matched-control or geo-test studies of a sponsorship** (Nielsen, Kantar, academic): [Not found]; one search returned only vendors' blogs on incrementality.
3. **An Indian cricket sponsor with published, measured sales lift:** [Not found]. Closest: Apollo told a trade title the deal "widened its dealer network and lifted brand awareness" (sponsorship.sportbusiness.com, January 2026; paywalled) [Reported by press]; Brand Finance, 9 June 2026: Apollo had the largest brand-strength increase among the world's top 25 tyre brands, no figure given (brandfinance.com/insights/apollo-tyres-going-the-distance) [Verified fact].
4. **Misattribution.** YouGov, 5 June 2026, eight weekly waves of IPL 2026 group-stage viewers: brands not sponsoring RCB were wrongly linked to RCB by 17.2% of viewers on average (CSK 11.2%, KKR 9.5%, PBKS 8.2%); correct linkage ranged from 58.5% for the best sponsor to 36.9%. [Verified fact] Recall context: an IPL 2025 tracker found two viewers in three recall a fantasy-sports brand, only a quarter of about 200 tracked brands crossed 4% recall, and a viewer recalls three to five brands a game (storyboard18.com). [Reported by press]

## 6. Rural event economics

- **LED wall hire** (a 2025-26 vendor guide, sparsadigital.in): Rs 50 to 150 per square foot a day by pixel pitch; a 200 sq ft (10 x 20 ft) wall Rs 12,000 to 30,000 a day basic, Rs 40,000 to 1 lakh premium; installation Rs 2,000 to 50,000; generator and transport extra (a generator Rs 8,000 to 15,000 a day in one city). One guide quotes Rs 1.2 to 2.5 lakh a day for a 20 x 10 ft stage set-up, which conflicts. Rural rates: [Not found]. [Vendor estimate]
- **Licensing.** A public screening is "communication to the public" and needs the rights holder's commercial licence (Copyright Act section 37, broadcast reproduction right); the Delhi High Court restrained cafes screening the 2014 World T20 without a licence; the ICC ran a public-screening application portal in 2019; the fee schedule: [Not found]. Police permission is case by case, with refusals on record (Kolkata 2012; Pune). Indore's smart-city body put two 10 x 20 ft screens in a market under a public-private arrangement for the 2026 T20 World Cup (knocksense.com). Whether a DD Free Dish feed shown at a dealer's shop escapes the licence is not established. [Fact - single source: a 2026 blog summary of the law; Reported by press for Indore]
- **Brands running rural match screenings at scale** (Hero, Castrol, Dream11, Jio): [Not found].

**Reading.** A dealer "match day" is cheap in hardware (a basic wall and generator for about Rs 25,000 to 45,000 a day) but not free in rights: screening at a shop needs the broadcaster's permission, which the McKinsey lens flagged and which remains open. [Interpretation]
