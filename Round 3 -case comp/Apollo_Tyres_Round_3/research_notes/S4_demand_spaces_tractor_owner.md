# S4. The tractor owner's demand spaces: occasions, needs, failure and advocacy (verified 7 Nov 2026)
Round-7 research note for the BCG-lens re-analysis of the Apollo Round 3 case. Feeds `..\BCG_Lens\C04` and `C03`.

**Scope.** The occasions on which an Indian tractor owner uses his tractor, the need in each, how each wears or fails a tyre, when and how the replacement is bought, and who advocates. Earlier notes hold the district hours studies, J.D. Power, warranty terms, the complaint and YouTube coding and the advice-source surveys; they are cited as "see 04 section x", "Q6 section x", "N3 Qx" and not redone. Method: 24 web searches, about 45 page fetches, four PDFs read. **Every URL below was fetched on 7 November 2026**; dates are as printed.

**Labels.** [Verified fact], [Fact - single source], [Reported by press], [Outside-in; not a survey], [Computed], [Interpretation]. Where nothing exists: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

## 7. A first demand-space map [Interpretation]

Occasions are defined by why, when and for what the tractor is used; the purchase moment (planned before a season, or on the day of failure) is a second axis across all of them. Evidence strength is our reading of sections 1 to 6.

## 9. Cautions and discrepancies

1. **Hours are tractor hours, not tyre purchases.** Nothing in section 1 measures tyre wear or spend by occasion; road hours and field hours wear differently.
2. **One state, one year, small samples.** Haryana is 200 owners in 2000; Tungabhadra 180 farmers in 2013-14 with no hours; Punjab seven centres; the groundnut-cotton figure has no visible state; 12 to 20% commercial is "by various estimates" and 45 to 55% is one unnamed analyst.
3. **Maker life claims differ by four to six times.** Apollo's guide says 10,000 to 15,000 hours; its other blog 1,500 to 2,500; a portal 2,000 to 4,000. None is a measurement; the larger figures read as generic international copy.
4. **Portal warranty entries contradict maker policy** (Apollo Farmking and Powerhaul "2" years against 7), and portal listings carry errors (a front tyre under rears; a 6.00-16 price with only a 6.50-16 card).
5. **Prices are asterisked list prices with no method**, differing by up to Rs 1,000 between portals on the same day; mandi and pair prices (N3 Q2) sit well below them.
6. **The cane fleet is an NGO's programme count** at 26 mills, ownership unstated; the overloading figures are police statements via one newspaper.
7. **Goodyear's farm share is about 30% (broker, 2022) or "an estimated 50%" (trade press, 2025)**; neither is audited, and the business was reported under review for sale.
8. **Digital figures mix company claims with modelled estimates** (40 lakh monthly users against 1.66 to 2.22 million); store bands are not users; one "Kubota" app is Thai.
9. **The Deoria study samples smartphone owners in one block** and ranks sources by score, not by share using each; the Jothilakshmi abstract gives no location.
10. **The pair-replacement evidence is a US forum and a portal guide**, not Indian dealer practice.
11. **Section 7 is interpretation.** The baseline survey (04 section 9) must add: hours by occasion last year, loads carried, cage-wheel use, and the month and trigger of the last tyre purchase.
