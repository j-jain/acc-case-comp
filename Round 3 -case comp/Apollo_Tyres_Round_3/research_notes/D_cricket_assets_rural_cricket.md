## 1. Team India lead sponsorship


### Q1 findings (batch 1)
- [Verified fact] BCCI announced Apollo Tyres as Lead Sponsor of the Indian men's and women's teams on 16 Sep 2025, replacing Dream11. BCCI's own release says the logo "will be featured on the jerseys" of both teams in all formats [S1, S3, S4]. Apollo's corporate page calls it a "3-year strategic partnership" (effective immediately), logo "on the front and leading arm" of the jersey [S2]. Press reports say ~2.5 years to March 2028 [S3, S5, S6].
- DURATION CONFLICT: "3-year" (Apollo/BCCI wording, exchange4media) vs "2.5 years to March 2028" (Business Standard, Onmanorama, Campaign India); Angel One/Nenow say 2027 [S8]. The brief's March 2028 matches the majority of press reports. Treat 3-year as approximate wording.
- [Single source / press report] Value Rs 579 crore: BCCI has NOT disclosed the amount, and Apollo's BSE filing reportedly did not state it [S5 Business Today via search snippet, S7]. Figure comes from Times of India-type "reports" (Campaign India attributes to Times of India). Treat as "reported by press", multiple outlets repeat it but trace to one origin.
- [Press report] Match count: 121 bilateral + 21 ICC = 142 matches [S4, S6, S7].
- [Press report] Per-match: BCCI base prices were Rs 3.5 cr per bilateral and Rs 1.5 cr per ICC match (Cricbuzz via Khel Now) [S7]. Headline "about Rs 4.77 crore per game" is an industry estimate; Khel Now's own check: 579/142 = Rs 4.08 cr, 579/121 = Rs 4.79 cr, so 4.77 appears to divide by bilateral matches only (arithmetic by Khel Now/this researcher, not an official fee). Other outlets: Rs 4.5 cr/match (Angel One, Cricket Addictor); Bridge Chronicle: Rs 4.77 cr bilateral and ~Rs 1.72 cr ICC. Official per-match fees: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
- [Press report] Bidders: Canva Rs 544 cr (some outlets Rs 554 cr) and JK Cement Rs 477 cr (Campaign India names "JK Tyre" - conflicts; most outlets say JK Cement). Shankh Air and Omniyat reportedly showed interest but did not qualify/bid [S9]. Dream11's previous deal reported at Rs 358 cr, July 2023 to March 2026 [S3, S7].
- [Verified fact] Dream11 exit: Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025 banned real-money gaming; BCCI ended the Dream11 deal early [S3, S4]. Men's team played Asia Cup 2025 (UAE) without a shirt sponsor [S3].
- [Press report] Tender: BCCI issued invitation 2 Sep 2025; barred categories: online/real-money gaming, betting, crypto, tobacco; reports add alcohol, sportswear/athleisure, banking/financial services, insurance, some others [S4, S7, S9]. The tender document itself was not found (Data unavailable).
- [Press report] First outings: men's logo debut in 2-Test home series vs West Indies from 2 Oct 2025; women's at ICC Women's World Cup from 30 Sep 2025; also Australia white-ball tour and India A vs Australia [S4 Campaign India].
- Apollo statement: Neeraj Kanwar: "national pride, strengthening consumer trust, and showcasing Apollo as a true leader in our category"; release promises "impactful fan engagement, innovative activations" [S2, S10]. Neither Apollo's page nor the Storyboard18 interview mentions farmers, rural or measurement.
- SCOPE CONFLICT (important): Apollo's corporate page lists "Title Sponsor Rights for all home international matches (men's and women's)" and "for domestic cricket tournaments organised by the BCCI" in addition to the jersey logo [S2]. No other source confirmed this; reports describe the deal only as jersey/lead sponsor across 121 bilateral + 21 ICC matches. Whether Apollo actually holds home-series title naming rights was not verified. Flag: "Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation."
- Rights NOT publicly itemised: training kit, imagery/group-image rules, "Official Lead Sponsor" designation wording, digital/content, tickets/hospitality, player access, India A/U-19. Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation. Categories below are separated:
  - Reported by press: logo on jersey front/leading arm, men's+women's, all formats, India A vs Australia mention.
  - Standard for BCCI lead sponsors historically (not verified for Apollo): kit/jersey sponsor typically also gets training-kit logo, official-sponsor designation, limited use of team marks in advertising via BCCI approval, hospitality tickets. ASSUMPTION until tender text is seen.
  - Not included (inference from structure, not stated): broadcast ad inventory (JioStar), IPL, ICC event marks/Global Partner rights, individual player endorsements.
