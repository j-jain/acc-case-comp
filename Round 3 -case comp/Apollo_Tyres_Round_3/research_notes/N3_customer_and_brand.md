# N3: The tractor tyre buyer in India and whose brand cricket builds (outside-in pass)

Researcher notes, second pass, compiled 6 Nov 2026. Research only: no solutions are proposed. Web searches used: 25 of the 40 allowed. Pages were fetched directly where the URL was known. YouTube data was read from YouTube's own page data in the in-app browser on 6 Nov 2026; this used no web searches.

Tag key: [Verified fact] = primary source read, or two independent sources; [Single source]; [Company claim]; [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]; [Computed]; [Interpretation]; [Unverified secondary claim].

Standing bias warning for every portal, YouTube or comment item below: the material is self-selected. It skews towards younger, online, Hindi-belt and Punjab/Haryana users. Some of it is paid or dealer-made content: several videos carry brand tags or dealer phone numbers, and commenters themselves call some videos ads. It is NOT representative of Indian tractor owners and must never be presented as a survey.

## Q1. Segments by value: farm versus commercial/haulage use, hours, renting out, trolleys

### Takeaway
No source gives a national split of tractor tyre demand by use type. The best available figures are small field surveys plus broker estimates of non-farm use (about 30-35%, unverified). The one internally detailed survey (Punjab cotton belt, 2016-17) shows tractor hours, and so probably tyre wear, concentrated in medium and large farms. That survey also shows that most tractors there were bought second-hand.

### Inferences
- If tyre wear tracks hours, medium and large owners (39% of tractor owners in the Punjab sample) account for roughly two-thirds of tyre-hours. Owners who hire out or haul add heavier wear per hour, because road and trolley work and stony loads drive wear complaints — [Interpretation]
- Second-hand tractors dominate among small owners (97% in Punjab). Their owners inherit whatever tyres the previous owner fitted, and they are more likely to replace on price — [Interpretation]
- Rough value ranking of owner types for replacement tyres, from the evidence above (interpretive, not measured): (1) haulage and custom-hire operators, (2) medium/large own-farm owners, (3) small owners of second-hand tractors, who buy cheap or second-hand — [Interpretation]

- National trolleys per tractor: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation. Only the Punjab household ratio above exists.

## Q2. Purchase occasions: urgent vs planned, pairs vs singles, rear/front/trailer, retreading and second-hand

### Takeaway
No study measures purchase occasions. Outside-in evidence shows a large, price-led "tyre mandi" channel (Agra, Patiala, Muktsar). These shops sell rear tyres as a pair (jodi) with a guarantee. There is heavy interest in second-hand and retreaded tyres. Warranty length and claim experience matter to buyers.

### Cited Findings
- J.D. Power TTSI 2018: 42% of owners received only a one-year tyre warranty. Satisfaction is highest (859) where the warranty is three years or more. When owners report uneven wear, repurchase and recommendation intent for the tyre brand fall to 18% and 22% — [J.D. Power TTSI 2018 release, 5/8 Oct 2018](https://www.jdpower.com/business/press-releases/jd-power-2018-india-tractor-tyre-satisfaction-index-ttsi-study) [Verified fact]
- J.D. Power quotes its practice lead on cutting "tyre replacement or retreading costs", which confirms retreading exists. No incidence is given — same source [Single source]
- Pairs: the most-viewed mandi videos price rear tyres per pair: "rear pair Rs 16,000 with guarantee, front pair Rs 2,500" (601,056 views, 18 Jun 2024) and "new tyre pair for Rs 22,000" (290,344 views, 29 Sep 2023), both from the Tractor Bajar channel in Agra. Commenters ask for pair prices ("joda ki kimat"), and one asks for 10 pairs — [Tractor Bajar IMCMmqelAjQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMCMmqelAjQ); [Tractor Bajar 0HOdxBNe6JQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0HOdxBNe6JQ) [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]
- Second-hand tyres: "Tyres from Rs 700, second hand tyre" has 1,597,066 views (Vikas ki videos, 29 Aug 2021). Others: a Punjabi video on new tractor tyres sold by weight at Rs 200/kg in Hoshiarpur (133,902 views); second-hand tractor and trolley tyres at "98% condition" (66,080 views); "Second hand tractor tyre at scrap rates" (22,876 views, Oct 2026) — [Bg5P0jnv0Y0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bg5P0jnv0Y0); [Njbfgihgq2o](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Njbfgihgq2o); [AEdqK1CxZH4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AEdqK1CxZH4); [8gxnpXgURjk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8gxnpXgURjk). All views as of 6 Nov 2026 [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]
- Remoulding: "Tractor tyre remodeling in Gadarwara" (MP) has 63,043 views, and commenters ask whether radials can be remoulded — [r-iatM8yDFM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-iatM8yDFM) [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]
- Retreading market: ICRA (via Tyre Trends) puts India's retreading market above Rs 60 billion, about 80% of it commercial vehicles. Tractors are not split out — [Tyre Trends](https://www.tyre-trends.com/spotlight/70-percent-truck-tyres-in-india-are-retreaded-once-icra) [Single source]
- Trailer tyres are sold only in replacement; tractor front and rear tyres sell through both OE and replacement (TechSci, dated 2009-13 data) — [The Machinist](https://www.themachinist.in/news/368/farm-tyre-market-grows-2009-techsci-research-report) (search summary) [Unverified secondary claim]
- CRISIL: replacement is nearly half of the roughly Rs 1 trillion Indian tyre industry's sales, all categories, FY26 outlook — [Business Standard, 18 Jul 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/tyre-industry-growth-forecast-fy26-crisil-125071800815_1.html) (search summary) [Single source]
- Guarantee as a selling point: dealer-channel videos lead with warranty years. Examples: "6 years full guarantee" for BKT (36,376 views, Sep 2026); "7 years full guarantee" for JK (45,248 views, Dec 2025); "Total 7 years warranty" for CEAT Aayushmaan (101,976 views, Mar 2020) — [J1fJ8F_om60](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1fJ8F_om60); [NsMTqTanWOQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NsMTqTanWOQ); [NJNDP1h7Fn0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NJNDP1h7Fn0) [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]

### Inferences
- The pair pricing on mandi channels suggests rear tyres are often replaced together. Whether that is the norm, or a shop pricing convention, is unknown — [Interpretation]
- Interest in second-hand and per-kg tyres (millions of views) suggests a large price-sensitive tier that new branded tyres do not serve — [Interpretation]

### Gaps
- Share of urgent (after failure) vs planned (pre-season) purchases: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
- Pair vs single purchase incidence; rear vs front vs trailer replacement frequency: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
- Incidence of retreaded or second-hand rear tyres on tractors: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

## Q3. Voice of the customer, outside-in [Outside-in, self-selected; not a survey]

### Takeaway
Tractor portals carry almost no usable tyre reviews. YouTube is where tyre talk happens. In 30 high-view Hindi and Punjabi videos, buyers mostly ask prices. Their brand opinions are polarised and argued from personal experience, chiefly tyre life in hours or years, cracking and cuts, and whether the warranty claim was honoured. MRF and Goodyear draw the most praise for life. Apollo draws many bare "Apollo best" votes but few reasons, and some cracking complaints. CEAT and JK draw mostly negative experience reports. "Dealer refused the claim" is a recurring complaint across MRF, JK, BKT and Apollo.

### Cited Findings

**3(a) Portals (viewed 6 Nov 2026)**
- TractorJunction tyre product pages (e.g. [Apollo Krishak Gold Drive 13.6x28](https://tractorjunction.com/apollo-tyres/krishak-gold-drive-13-6-x-28/16)) have no user-review section, no price ("Get the best price") and no OE statement — [Verified fact]

- KhetiGaadi /tyres and TractorGyan /used-tractor returned 404. 91trucks tyre-review pages returned HTTP 410 (gone) — [Verified fact]
- Tyreplex is the only portal with a count: MRF Shakti Super has an average of 4.1 from 102 ratings and 45 reviews (the header says 114). The three visible reviews carry no content ("very nice", "Mast", "First time"; 1-5 stars; dated Dec 2020 and Oct 2026) — [Tyreplex](https://www.tyreplex.com/mrf-tyres/shakti-super) [Single source]. Tyreplex URLs for Apollo, Goodyear, CEAT and BKT tractor lines redirect to car-tyre brand pages.
- Portal review counts and ratings for Apollo Krishak/Virat/Farm King, Goodyear Vajra Super/Sampurna, CEAT Aayushmaan/Vardhan, BKT Commander and JK Sona/Shresth: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

## Q7. Who the tractor owner is

### Takeaway
Hard numbers on owner age, education and phone use are scarce. J.D. Power shows younger owners (under 35) are harder to please, cross-shop more and show weaker brand loyalty. Field studies show owners skew to larger farms, and that small owners mostly buy second-hand.

### Cited Findings
- J.D. Power 2018 Tractor PPI (released 7 Aug 2018; n=3,835; 14 states; Jan-May 2018): owners under 35 are 20 points less satisfied. 63% of them would "definitely" recommend their brand vs 68% of older owners. Cross-shopping rose from 14% (2015) to 21% (2018). 58% would definitely repurchase the same brand (52% in 2017) — [J.D. Power PPI 2018](https://www.jdpower.com/business/press-releases/2018-india-tractor-product-performance-index-ppi-study) [Verified fact]. The share of owners under 35 is not published.
- In the same release, J.D. Power says younger rural cohorts are "increasingly less influenced by word-of-mouth" and evaluate products themselves — same [Single source; interpretation by J.D. Power]
- Purchase factors for tractors, Adilabad, Telangana (survey Jan-Mar 2020, Garrett ranking): fuel efficiency first, then subsidy and maintenance cost. "Recommendations by relatives/friends/local mechanics/dealers" ranks 6th. Most farmers preferred 40-50 HP — [Kaustub et al. 2020, AJAEES 38(9)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ags/ajaees/357870.html) [Single source]
- Punjab cotton belt: tractor ownership rises from 24.6% of marginal to 100% of medium and large households; 64% of owners bought second-hand — [Kaur 2024](https://jndmeerut.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/16.pdf) [Single source]
- Digital behaviour: an industry blog says Sonalika got 32% of enquiries from digital in one campaign — [Tractor For Everyone, Jan 2026](https://www.tractorforeveryone.com/knowledge-center/how-social-media-is-driving-tractor-sales-in-rural-india) [Company claim, via blog]. TractorGyan reports 280.5K YouTube subscribers and 792.8K Facebook followers (its site footer, 6 Nov 2026) — [Single source]

### Inferences
- High-view YouTube tyre content suggests a sizeable online audience of tractor users in the Hindi belt and Punjab, but view counts are not owner counts — [Interpretation]
