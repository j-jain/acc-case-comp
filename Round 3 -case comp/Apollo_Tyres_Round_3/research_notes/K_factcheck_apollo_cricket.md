## Corrections needed

- **Claim 4 (PARTLY):** use "~6,500 dealerships" and tractor 6%, but note the CRISIL sentence reads "tractor and light commercial segment 6% and 9%, respectively" (paired reading: tractor 6%, LCV 9%; T&B 55%, PV 22%). Safer: "CRISIL (14 Aug 2026) puts tractor tyres at about 6% of domestic sales, in line with Apollo's own 6% standalone farm share."
- **Claim 22 (CORRECTION):** quoted phrase is exact, but replace "grew strongly, especially in the second half" with: domestic farm replacement "delivered strong growth", "supported by a recovery in demand during the second half of the fiscal" (FY26 AR).
- **Claim 14 (CORRECTION):** keep the figures, but Rs 579 cr is a reported (Times of India) figure; BCCI did not disclose it. Third bidder is JK Cement (CricTracker, Storyboard18); Campaign India says "JK Tyre" and is dated 19 Sep 2025, so do not cite it for the bidders. Apollo's own page says "Three years" and lists title sponsor rights for home internationals (men's and women's) and BCCI domestic tournaments; it gives no value, match count or date.
- **Claim 15 (CORRECTION):** no longer press-only: BCCI official release 26 Sep 2026 (per Business Today). Scope reported as all international series and tournaments hosted in India plus BCCI domestic competitions; value Rs 130-150 cr (Rs 4.3-4.85 cr per match, ~35 matches), one outlet up to Rs 171 cr (snippet only). This overlaps Apollo's listed home-international/domestic title rights; flag it and explain (Apollo is "lead sponsor", Muthoot "title sponsor" of home cricket).
- **Claim 13 (PARTLY):** drop "registered by calling its call centre within a month of purchase" for farm tyres; it is not on Apollo's farm/agri warranty pages (the one-month line is from a passenger-tyre page, snippet only). CEAT's 7 years applies to tractor tyres made on/after 1 Jan 2019 (5 years before); its page does not say "farm tyres".
- **Claim 19 (PARTLY):** attribute to a Motilal Oswal note of 27 Dec 2025 written after meeting Apollo management; "farm category" is not explicitly Apollo's or the industry's. Do not present as an industry statistic.
- **Claim 20:** results are out (4 Nov 2026): revenue Rs 7,968 cr (+16.7%), net profit Rs 292 cr (+13.3%), operating margin ~11% (aggregator figure; official not opened). No farm/rural/cricket comment found yet.
- **Claim 17 (minor):** the 1st SL ODI venue was later moved from Delhi to Visakhapatnam per a related BCCI story on the same page; avoid quoting Delhi.
- **Claim 3 (precision):** Rs 1,190 cr is correct arithmetic, but 6% is rounded; say "about Rs 1,200 cr (6%, rounded)".
- **Claim 12 (context):** the 2019 "8-10%" is a historical statement; the investor-presentation share is 5-6% standalone today. Do not set them side by side as a trend without explaining.
- **Claim 16 (minor):** CEAT's Rs 240 cr is for "certain segments"; its separate timeout deal is also ~Rs 240 cr over five years.
- **Claim 7 (context):** rural reach is tied first to car and two-wheeler tyres; "agri and truck" is the add-on. Exact date 14 Nov 2025.
