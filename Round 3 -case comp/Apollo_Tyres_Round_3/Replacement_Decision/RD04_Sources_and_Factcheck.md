## 2. Fact-check, round 8

Run on 8 November 2026 on RD00 to RD03 as first written; the full table, with the source URL and the wording found for each claim, is `..\research_notes\V_factcheck_round8.md`. Twenty-six groups of claims, split into 34 rows where a claim cites several sources, were read at the source, together with a spot-check of 19 rows of the owner-voice corpus in the browser, a recount of the corpus from the CSV, and a recomputation of every total in RD02 section 6 and RD03 section 3.

| Verdict | Count | What it covered |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | 21 | J.D. Power's 2018 tractor-tyre, 2017 US, 2015 Japan and 2022 US figures; AutoPacific; Modern Tire Dealer 2011 and 2003; CEAT's 2020 afaqs lines; Mohanty and Patro; the DAESI line; the Kurnool figures; Autobei; the Xoxoday case; India Cements 2023; Asian Paints; Kelly, Presslee and Webb; Herrmann and others; section 194R; Apollo's call lines; the calendar (no home international announced between 4 March 2027 and January 2028; England's tour appears only in a broadcaster's rights list and press tables) |
| Confirmed with a correction | 8 | The 2016 farm-tyre price line (one wholesaler); the CCI line (CEAT's submission recorded in the order, not a Commission finding); Rajkot's "recommendations" (any source); Bridgestone ("certain milestones"; athlete appearances for a fee); Firestone (sales associates; "boosting its sales between 30% and 50% annually"); Gulf Oil (a relocated home match, 2018); the Tamil Nadu Act's wording; the Apollo marketing head's "plan" line |
| Partly confirmed | 3 | The 2026 US loyalty reason (the release does not say whether wear is the reason for replacing or for leaving); the Modern Tire Dealer 2025 poll's basis (dealers' "most often" answers, so shop shares are 72 to 78%, not 72 to 92%); India Cements 2018 (a consumer contest through dealers, not a trade reward) |
| Not confirmed | 2 | "Most buyers trust their mechanics and dealers, who are still very influential" is the Adgully interviewer's question, not CEAT's answer; "decisions are often dealer-led" is Social Samosa's writer, not BKT's CMO |
| Could not check | 0 | – |

**Corpus recount.** 261 rows; self 159, tyre dealer 22, tractor dealer 15, video 10, peer 4, mechanic 1, unknown 50; LIFE 109; the tractor dealer in 14 of 44 after-failure items: all as stated. MRF is named in 76 rows, not 75. All 15 requested items exist at their links with accurate excerpts; three codings are doubtful (the bat item names no decider; two comments by one dealer are a dealer's view, not a replacement decision). Rows YT001 to YT057 link to the video, not the comment. Fourteen of the 22 "tyre dealer" rows are dealers' own videos and comments; owners name a dealer in 8 items (3%), and in none does the dealer pick the brand.

**Arithmetic.** Every line of RD02 section 6.1 (Rs 0.98 crore), the slab test (54%), the national slabs (Rs 2.8 crore; about 4,740 pairs; about half a point), RD03's +0.2 and -0.7 and both ranges, and the pull-through ceilings (45%, 30%, 22.5%) are correct. Two figures were wrong and are corrected: RD02 section 6.2's net (about +0.1 crore, +0.2 with the intercept, not +0.3 to 0.5) and RD01 section 5's all-replacement range (35 to 41% owner-led, 59 to 65% trade-led). "Break-even stays near 1.3 points" held only before the slabs; with them at scale it is about 1.7 unless they pay for themselves. The Bain budget carries Rs 2.2 crore for four cricket parts that add to Rs 2.73 crore, so the savings lines in RD03 carry a caution.

**Corrections applied on 8 November 2026.** The 28 corrections listed in V section 5 were applied to RD00, RD01, RD02 and RD03; the matching fixes in V section 6 were applied to U1 (rows 1.1, 2.8, 2.9, 4.1, section 5 reading, "what matters most" items 3 and 5, caution 3), U2 (the link note, MRF's count, the one-dealer comments, the bat item), U3 (Gulf Oil, India Cements) and U4 (Rajkot). The main consequence for the argument: the Indian makers' statements that the trade decides are weaker than first written, which leaves the trade-led estimate resting on shop surveys abroad, CEAT's 2020 line about the mechanic at a failure, the CCI record and the farm-input analogue. The estimate is unchanged; its evidence is thinner and is labelled so.

**Claims the round did not reach.** About twenty secondary claims (V section 7), among them the dealer counts from Q4, the Champions Offer terms, BKT's eight IPL teams and CEAT's other dealer schemes; and the roughly 240 corpus rows not opened.
