## 1. Rural cricket audience 2022-2026

### Takeaway
No BARC or broadcaster rural/urban or state split for 2022-2026 India matches was retrievable; the only rural-vs-urban BARC split found is still the 2017 women's final (already known). New usable facts: Ormax 2024 sizes the cricket audience (612 mn, urban+rural sample of 12,000) but its public page hides the rural cut; DD Free Dish reaches about 49 mn homes (Prasar Bharati FY2024-25); JioStar says Hindi IPL 2025 grew slowest.

### Cited Findings
- [Single source, survey, medium-high] Ormax Sports Audience Report 2024, published 20 Mar 2024, fieldwork Nov 2023-Jan 2024, 12,000 respondents across urban and rural India: sports audience 678 mn, cricket audience 612 mn (football 305 mn, kabaddi 280 mn; another Ormax summary gives football 280 mn, so football is uncertain). Audience = watched sports on TV/OTT in the past year for 30+ minutes. Page says the report profiles gender, age, NCCS, population strata and states, but publishes no rural, state, age or occupation numbers (full data on request). Denominator is all Indians (47.9% of population for 678 mn), not rural. — [Ormax](https://ormaxmedia.com/insights/stories/678-million-sports-audiences-in-india-game-on.html)
- [Verified fact, BARC via trade press, 2017, old] Women's World Cup 2017 final: rural 9.9 mn vs urban 9.5 mn impressions; only match where rural beat urban; total 19.53 mn; BARC began rural measurement in 2015. — [Medianews4u, 5 Aug 2017](https://www.medianews4u.com/ind-vs-eng-womens-world-cup-match-attracts-19-53-million-impressions/)
- [Single source, broadcaster self-report] IPL 2025: JioStar says Hindi feed viewership grew 31%, slowest of the top five language feeds (Telugu +87%, Kannada +65%, Tamil +52%). Opening weekend TV reach 25.3 crore per BARC, universe "TG 2+ All India Urban+Rural"; no rural share published. Taken from search summaries of trade press, pages not individually fetched. — [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/cricket/ipl-2025-jiohotstar-shatters-viewership-records-on-historic-opening-weekend-check-full-details)
- [Verified fact] DD Free Dish: about 49 million homes, 381 TV channels and 48 radio channels, FY2024-25 revenue about Rs 1,060.71 crore, per Prasar Bharati's annual report as reported 3 Apr 2026. Article calls rural and price-sensitive reach its "defining advantage" but gives no rural household count. — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/media-and-entertainment/dd-free-dish-nears-rs1060-crore-in-revenue-maintains-scale-of-49-million-homes-94090.htm); corroborated by FICCI-EY via [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/dd-freedish-reaches-49-mn-homes-youtube-grows-63-of-tvs-reach-in-india-ficci-ey-report-142163.html) (49 mn) and a Lok Sabha reply cited by Dinamalar (33 mn in 2018 to 49 mn in 2024).
- [Unverified secondary claim] Higher Free Dish estimates: 50-60 mn (Impact on Net), 60+ mn (a 2025 table attributed to Chrome DM/ET). India total TV households about 210-230 mn (Scatmag; IANS Nov 2025). TRAI: active DTH subscribers fell from 62.17 mn (Jun 2024) to 56.07 mn (Jun 2025). Older BARC universe: 77.5 mn urban and 76 mn rural TV homes (afaqs, c.2016). — [Impact on Net](https://dev.impactonnet.com/backbeat/impact-of-free-dishs-growing-importance-on-the-indian-television-ecosystem-8637.html)
- [Unverified secondary claim, year mismatch] An Impact on Net article cites 125 mn reach for a first India-Pakistan Asia Cup match and 200 mn first-week cumulative reach, but the 28 August date matches the 2022 edition, not 2025. — [Impact on Net](https://www.impactonnet.com/more-from-impact/asia-cup-breaks-all-records-on-television-8011.html)

### Inferences
- Interpretation: Free Dish (about 49 mn homes) is large enough that a DD Sports carriage of India matches reaches rural homes, but no measured rural audience exists in what was found.
- Interpretation: the "Hindi grew slowest" IPL 2025 line is a growth rate, not a share; it does not show Hindi-belt rural viewership is low.

### Gaps
- BARC/broadcaster rural-urban or state splits for India matches, 2023 ODI WC, 2024 T20 WC, 2025 Champions Trophy, IPL 2023-26: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation (exchange4media's BARC week pages returned 403).
- Hindi-market share of viewership: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
- DD Sports viewership in Asia Cup 2025 weeks: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.
- Cricket following by age and occupation (middle-aged village landowners): Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation. YouGov, Kantar, Lokniti-CSDS, Axis My India cuts not found.

## 2. Grassroots and rural cricket ecosystem

### Takeaway
CricHeroes is huge but metro-led and publishes no small-town or village split; 68.6% of its 2025 matches were tennis-ball. Government rural festivals are the only place with verified village-scale cricket participation numbers (Rajasthan 2022: about 30 lakh registered across six sports; Kurukshetra 2025: 3.30 lakh; Sansad Khel Mahakumbh: tens of thousands).

### Cited Findings
- [Single source, company self-report, 19 Mar 2026, data for calendar 2025] CricHeroes: 4,872,624 matches scored (about 13,350/day; busiest month December, 573,249); tennis-ball 3,342,351 (68.6% of total, my arithmetic) vs leather-ball 1,328,755; tournaments 395,520 (avg 8 matches); grounds 187,779; active players 12,380,543; active teams 1,960,338; newly registered cricketers 10,356,442; average 8.44 matches per active player; active players aged 40+ 183,459 (about 1.5% of active players, my arithmetic); streamed matches 6,106 across 689 tournaments; 142 countries, 7,315 cities; Delhi NCR top (329,505 matches), then Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad. Page reports nothing on small towns or villages. — [CricHeroes blog](https://blog.cricheroes.com/?p=11809)
- [Conflict, low reliability] Search snippets of earlier CricHeroes summaries give 30 mn registered and 11.6 mn matches for 2024 and a different 37.2 mn registered figure, versus 4.87 mn matches in 2025: definitions differ, so year-on-year comparison is unsafe. Not fetched.
- [Single source, press 2023] CricHeroes village-level cricketer subscription Rs 99 per month / Rs 499 per year. — [The Week, 6 Apr 2023](https://www.theweek.in/news/sports/2023/04/06/crickheroes-an-app-to-identify-and-nurture-hidden-talents-of-cricket.html)
- [Single source, government claim] Rajasthan Rajiv Gandhi Gramin Olympic Games 2022: 29 Aug-5 Oct 2022, gram panchayat level, six sports (kabaddi, tennis-ball cricket, volleyball, shooting volleyball, kho kho, hockey); about 30 lakh registrations and 2 lakh+ teams. — [The Bridge](https://thebridge.in/olympics/rajasthan-rajiv-gandhi-rural-olympics-massive-response-34763) (no per-sport breakdown). A search summary (not verified) says one report put cricket at 6.88 lakh of 29.14 lakh; source and edition unclear.
- [Single source, pre-event government claim] 2023 edition (rural + urban): 54.70 lakh registered, 42.28 lakh rural and 12.43 lakh urban; urban tennis-ball cricket 2.74 lakh (2.52 lakh men, 22,590 women). No rural tennis-ball figure found. Via search summary of The Statesman / Free Press Journal; pages not fetched.
- [Single source] Sansad Khel Mahotsav, Kurukshetra Lok Sabha constituency (MP Naveen Jindal): over 3.30 lakh registered (headline 3.28 lakh); cricket included; block level 14-22 Nov, assembly level 1-6 Dec, Lok Sabha level 15-24 Dec (2025, article dated 5 Nov 2025); Rs 2 crore prize money announced. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/over-3-30lakh-register-for-sansad-khel-mahotsav-in-kurukshetra-lok-sabha-constituenc)
- [Single source] Sansad Khel Mahakumbh (Anurag Thakur, Hamirpur HP): edition 1.0 about 42,700 youth, 1,400+ teams; edition 2.0 (Jan 2023, Basti UP phase) 2,300 teams, 45,700+ youth; edition 3.0 (Mar 2024) target 75,000 players, reports of 800+ panchayats and 5,000+ villages; prize money reported inconsistently (Rs 20 lakh vs Rs 50 lakh). — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/sports/anurag-singh-thakur-inaugurates-sansad-khel-mahakumbh-30-bats-with-rohit-sharma-772114)
- [Single source] NaMo Tennis Ball Cricket League 2026, final phase 28 Sep-4 Oct 2026 at Tau Devi Lal Stadium, Panchkula: prizes Rs 21 lakh winner, Rs 11 lakh runner-up, Rs 5 lakh third, Rs 2.5 lakh fourth; teams qualified through earlier stages nationwide; organiser not named in article. — [UNI](https://www.uniindia.com/north-india/sports-tennis-cricket-haryana/193135)
- [Single source, already known] Sirsa/Fatehabad village tournaments: winner Rs 1.70 lakh, runner-up Rs 1.21 lakh, live social-media coverage credited, no viewer counts. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/cricket-craze-strengthens-community-bond-in-sirsa-fatehabad-villages/)
- [Unverified/low relevance] A 2022 Gujarat fake "IPL" streamed on YouTube to deceive bettors shows that unverified viewer claims for village streams need scepticism. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/fake-ipl-in-gujarat-how-gujarat-villagers-tricked-russian-gamblers-betting-on-fake-ipl-matches-ipl-news-indian-premier-league-news-208626)

### Inferences
- Interpretation: tennis-ball cricket is the dominant format on the one app with scale, and the 40+ cohort is tiny on it (about 1.5%), so the app is not a route to middle-aged landowners.
- Interpretation: Rajasthan 2022 and Kurukshetra 2025 show cricket sits inside multi-sport political festivals with registration claims in lakhs; these are registrations, not turnout or audience.

## 4. Official ownership tables

### Takeaway
The official portal structure was located, but its dynamic tables could not be pulled, so there is still no official tractor-by-size-class figure. Size-class shares of holdings and area for the 2015-16 census came from a secondary table.

### Cited Findings
- [Verified fact, portal structure] The Agriculture Census site hosts Input Survey 2016-17 data (agricultural equipment/machines among its topics) at databasehomei.aspx, and all-India tables at nationaltables.aspx, nationalsummary.aspx and statesummary_sizegroups.aspx. The pages are menus; no tractor table values were returned. — [Agriculture Census portal](https://agcensus.da.gov.in/databasehomei.aspx)
- [Proxy evidence, secondary table, medium-low] Agriculture Census 2015-16 all-India (% of holdings / % of operated area): marginal (<1 ha) 68.45 / 24.03; small (1-2 ha) 17.62 / 23.54; semi-medium (2-4 ha) 9.43 / 23.61; medium (4-10 ha) 3.76 / 19.96; large (10 ha and above) 0.57 / 8.85. Variants for combined groups: small+marginal 86.21% of holdings and 47.34% of area (one source) vs 86.08% and 46.94% (another); large 9.07% of area in one source vs 8.85%. Provisional vs final differences likely. — [IASpoint table](https://iaspoint.com/?p=507923) (via search summary; must be checked against the official report, Business Standard/PTI of Oct 2018 returned 403)
- [Single source] NSS 77th round Situation Assessment (NSO/MoSPI, published Sept 2021, survey year 2018-19): about 93.094 mn agricultural households (54% of rural households); 70.4% of agricultural households possess under 1 ha; 0.4% over 10 ha. No tractor figure retrieved. — [PARI library](https://ruralindiaonline.org/en/library/resource/situation-assessment-of-agricultural-households-and-land-and-livestock-holdings-of-households-in-rural-india/)
- [Unverified secondary claim] Only 4.4% of agricultural households owned tractors as of June 2018 (NCAER, likely derived from the NSS survey); the NCAER page fetched did not contain it, so unverified. — [NCAER](https://ncaer.org/?p=20151)
- [Unverified secondary claim] Average age of an Indian operational holder about 50 years (50.1 in 2016), attributed to the All India Report on Input Survey; found only via a blog; official report not retrieved. — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/blog/india/farmers-ageing-new-generation-disinterested-who-will-grow-our-food--65800)
- [Single source, state, off-topic for all-India] Goa's Input Survey 2016-17 report PDF is image-heavy; no readable tractor table. — [Goa DPSE](https://www.dpse.goa.gov.in/Report on Input Survey 2016-17.pdf)

### Inferences
- Interpretation: with 86% of holdings under 2 ha and the 2009 IFPRI pattern (38% of over-10 ha holders own tractors, under 1% under 2 ha) the tractor-owning base is concentrated in the roughly 14% of holdings above 2 ha holding about 53% of area; this combination is mine and rests on a secondary table.

### Gaps
- Input Survey 2016-17 all-India tractors by size class; NSS 77th round tractor ownership; official farmer age: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation (needs manual download from the portal or the Input Survey report PDF).

## 5. Warranty facts from manufacturer documents

### Takeaway
Resolved: Apollo's own India warranty page gives farm tyres 7 years from the week of manufacture, pro-rata on tread, for production from July 2018 onward. The "2 years" on portals is stale or wrong. MRF's own page gives 6 years. Goodyear, CEAT, JK and BKT farm terms were not available from official pages.

### Cited Findings
- [Verified fact, primary] Apollo Tyres India warranty page, row "Farm tyres": "7 years from the week of Manufacturing"; footnote "July 2018 Production onwards"; "Pro-rata (based on Tread depth)"; replacement pro rata "based on the Tread Wear Indicator"; cover ends at TWI exposure or end of period, whichever is earlier. Page names "Farm tyres", not "tractor tyres". Original retail purchaser only, non-transferable, proof of purchase required; exclusions include road hazard, improper mounting, under-inflation, overloading, unauthorised repairs, use outside India; taxes, mounting, inflation borne by customer; no minimum mileage guaranteed; terms changeable without notice; India only. Page undated; fetched 6 Nov 2026. — [Apollo](https://www.apollotyres.com/en-in/support/warranty/)
- [Verified fact, primary] MRF Limited Warranty Policy: farm and OTR tyres "6 years from the month of Manufacturing on the tyre", pro-rata, "Upto 85 % of Tread depth"; MRF may modify without notice; page undated (footer 2026). — [MRF](https://mrftyres.com/warranty-policy)
- [Unverified secondary claim, low reliability, corroborating Apollo] TractorGyan Q&A 26 Jul 2025: Apollo 7 years pro-rata from week of manufacture, July 2018 onward. — [TractorGyan](https://tractorgyan.com/tractors-question-hub/how-long-is-the-warranty-on-apollo-tractor-tyres/474). The "2 years" figure appears in TractorGyan's rear-tyre section and TractorKarvan listings (e.g. FX 515, Farmking 12.4-28): conflicts with Apollo's own page. — [TractorKarvan](https://tractorkarvan.com/tractor-tyres/apollo-12-4-28-farmking)
- [Unverified secondary claim, portal Q&A] Goodyear tractor tyres: 6 years from manufacture or until 85% tread worn, whichever first (post 14 May 2025). — [TractorGyan](https://tractorgyan.com/tractors-question-hub/how-long-is-the-warranty-on-good-year-tractor-tyres/425). MRF 6 years (post 17 Mar 2025) matches MRF's own page. JK Sona-1 front tyre 7 years (comparison listing) and Ralco up to 3 years, MRL 7 years, Birla Farm Haul Platina 5 years: portal claims only. — [TractorGyan JK](https://tractorgyan.com/tractors-question-hub/how-long-is-the-warranty-period-on-jk-tractor-tyres/389)
- [Verified fact, primary, partial] JK Tyre warranty statement page: tyres "warranted against manufacturing defects arising out of any deficiency in design"; claims replaced "on pro-rata wear based on percentage of tread wear"; period appears on a separate page not retrieved. — [JK Tyre](https://jktyre.com/warrantypolicy.aspx)
- [Verified fact, primary, partial] CEAT warranty page has a tab "for Tractor, Farm and OTR Tyres" but no term was extractable. — [CEAT](https://www.ceat.com/warranty.html). Goodyear official policy page (p=2398) returned 404 on fetch; a search snippet says it covers all Goodyear India tyres except racing with no farm-specific terms.

### Inferences
- Interpretation: Apollo's 7 years from manufacture is the longest of the brands checked (MRF 6, Goodyear 6 claimed), but all are pro-rata, end at 85%/TWI wear, and run from manufacture rather than purchase, so effective cover is shorter than the headline.

### Gaps
- Official farm-tyre terms for CEAT, BKT, JK (period) and Goodyear: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

## 6. Tractor-tyre life and replacement cycle

### Takeaway
Quoted lives range from 1,500-2,500 hours (Apollo blog, well-maintained) to 2,000-4,000 hours (TractorGyan) and 4-6 years; no ICAR or university measurement was found. Tractor-trailer stock is not directly reported; MoRTH trailers are about 2.16 mn.

### Cited Findings
- [Manufacturer claim, low reliability] Apollo blog: "On average, well-maintained tractor tyres can last anywhere between 1,500 to 2,500 hours of use"; front and rear wear differently but no separate figures; undated (footer 2026). — [Apollo Tyres](https://www.apollotyres.com/en-in/stories/blogs/tyre-guides/maintenance-tips-to-improve-the-life-of-tractor-tyres/)
- [Portal claim, low reliability] TractorGyan Q&A (25 Mar 2026): "a solid 2,000 to 4,000 hours", up to 5,000 on soft soil or grass, lower on asphalt or rock; a blog on the site says 4-6 years on average, condition over age. — [TractorGyan](https://tractorgyan.com/tractors-question-hub/how-many-hours-do-tractor-tyres-last/580)
- [Single source, dataset aggregator] MoRTH-derived Dataful table: about 21,63,551 registered trailers (31 Mar 2022) and about 1.01 crore tractors; category groups tractor-trolleys and agricultural trailers, so the count may under- or over-state trolleys. — [Dataful](https://dataful.in/datasets/23333/)

### Inferences
- Interpretation: at 300-500 operating hours a year (my assumption, not sourced) 1,500-4,000 hours implies roughly 4-10 years; usage hours per year were not sourced, so do not cite this as a finding.

### Gaps
- ICAR/agricultural-university or tractor-maker statement of tyre life; front vs rear lives; trolley counts: Data unavailable – requires primary research or company confirmation.

## 7. ICC rule on team-sponsor logos at ICC events

### Takeaway
The ICC regulation text was not retrieved. Press evidence shows the tournament logo is mandatory on team shirts and, for the 2023 World Cup, Dream11 stayed on the chest above the "INDIA" wordmark while the manufacturer logo moved.

- [Single source] ICC source (Champions Trophy 2025): "It is the responsibility of every team to add the tournament logo to their jerseys"; all teams obliged. — [WION](https://www.wionews.com/cricket/india-champions-trophy-news-teams-are-obligated-to-have-host-logo-says-icc-india-vs-pakistan-jersey-logo-row-bcci-secretary-saikia-says-will-follow-rules-8644565)
- [Single source, unverified] A search summary says India moved its sponsor logo to the right sleeve at the WTC final because sponsorship space is limited at ICC events; also, ICC later allowed a centre front-of-shirt sponsor logo in Tests. Pages not fetched. — [CricTracker](https://crictracker.com/icc-allows-teams-to-display-front-of-shirt-sponsor-logos-in-tests)
- [Proxy evidence, old] ICC clothing regulations copies (2013 era) define commercial logo and require ICC approval. — [ICC regulations copy](https://mumbaicricket.com/download/ICC/regulations_advertising_clothing.pdf)

### Inferences
- Interpretation: the principal sponsor typically keeps a chest position at ICC events, but this is based on one kit report, not the regulation.

### Gaps
