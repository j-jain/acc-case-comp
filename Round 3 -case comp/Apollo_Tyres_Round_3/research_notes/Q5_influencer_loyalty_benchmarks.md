# Q5. Best demonstrated practice: programmes that pay the adviser (7 Nov 2026)

Round-6 research note for the Bain-lens re-analysis. Web research, 30 searches and page reads, run 7 November 2026. Feeds `..\Bain_Lens\B05_Commercial_Excellence_Route_to_Market.md`.

**Why this note exists.** Bain's benchmarking tool is "best demonstrated practice": find who does the thing best, anywhere, and copy the mechanism. A tractor tyre is chosen on the word of a dealer or a mechanic, like paint on a painter's word, cement on a mason's, lubricant on a mechanic's. The earlier files cited Fevicol and Castrol once each without design detail. This note collects the designs and the few numbers that exist.

## 3. Design rules the evidence supports

1. **Verify the adviser before paying him.** A KYC database with unique IDs, and points credited only on a sale the dealer authenticates by SMS or app, or on a scanned pack code (the mjunction masons' case; Masterstrokes' QR per bucket; Shell's pack codes). The masons' programme went from 4,662 to 13,347 members and +326% attributed bags in a year on that backbone. [Benchmark - external]
2. **Credit within 24 hours and deliver to the doorstep.** The rebar case syncs points nightly and couriers rewards into the hinterland; its active share rose from 40% to 72%. Berger's headline perk is "instant cash transfers". [Benchmark - external; Verified fact]
3. **Points pay for the sale; insurance, health cover and scholarships buy the relationship.** Shell Samriddhi, JK Uphaar (10,000+ policies), Berger Lift and Win, Pidilite's Sanjeevani (a family of six), Fevicol's Shiksha Samman. Every programme that has lasted a decade carries a family-welfare layer. [Verified fact; Fact - single source]
4. **Certify, do not only train.** ASDC-certified Castrol mechanics, Ambuja's "certification and empanelment", Berger ID cards, JCB-certified operators. A certificate raises the adviser's own price and ties his credential to the brand. [Fact - single source]
5. **Make recognition public and competitive.** Castrol's contest drew 1.27 lakh entrants; Amaron's gives motorcycles to city champions; Ambuja runs talent hunts; Fevicol's free-labour day set a record. Contests recruit at a scale points schemes do not. [Fact - single source]
6. **Budget on active advisers, not enrolment.** JK Advantage had 35% engagement yet members gave 80% of purchases; the rebar case tracked 72% active; vendors call 20 to 30% active the norm. [Benchmark - external; Vendor estimate]
7. **Reach feature-phone advisers by IVR and missed call in local languages** (Castrol in nine languages; Asian Paints' radio programme in seven, onboarding 11,000 painters), with an app only for the smartphone tier. [Fact - single source]
8. **Turn the outlet into a claims-and-service point.** JK's rural Steel Wheels settle claims instantly on non-truck tyres; CEAT Shoppes settle on the spot and collect NPS; UltraTech has 6,280 stores. For a low-trust purchase the visible settlement of a claim is the trust signal the buyer can see. [Fact - single source; Verified fact]
9. **Field cadence beats media.** Hero's rural vertical and camps in 100,000+ villages took rural from about 42-43% to 45% of its volumes in two years, and by press reports not verified at source to about half by 2017-18; Castrol's 40-city mechanic events; Gulf's six-month van tours. Where cricket appears (Castrol's Champions Trophy launch and IPL tie-up; Gulf's cricketer-fronted training), it opens the door, and the mechanic is assigned the conversion. [Fact - single source each]
10. **Plan a two-to-three-year ramp.** Ambuja 70,000 to 120,000 contractors in two years; the masons' case +186% in year two; the rebar case 5x volume over 2022 to 2024. Year-one figures look small in every case. [Benchmark - external; Fact - single source]
