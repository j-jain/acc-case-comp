## 10. Cautions and discrepancies

1. **Utilisation** differs by source and date: decks 91% (Q1) and 92% (H1 FY27) for India; the CFO "almost 85%, 90%" on 5 Nov 2026; CRISIL "~90%" for FY26; ICICI-A "> 80%" in February 2026. Name the source each time.
2. **Net debt** is Rs 3,300 crore on the company's definition (30 Sep 2026), Rs 4,525 crore on Stockanalysis (with leases), and 0.93x EBITDA on CRISIL's definition (with dealer deposits and leases) against the company's 0.4x; CRISIL prints gearing as both 0.32x and 0.33x.
3. **P/E** is 12.8x (Screener) and 15.1x (Stockanalysis) for the same day because the earnings bases differ; EV/EBITDA is 8.04x (Stockanalysis) and 7.2x (Value Research, undated).
4. **The 2021 net debt in the decomposition is a planning assumption** (Rs 5,000 crore, range 4,400 to 5,900); it moves the multiple and deleveraging legs by about 1 point each way; the sum is unchanged.
5. **Rival EBITDA** for FY21 and FY26 is rebuilt from Screener's rounded margins (CAGRs about plus or minus 1 point); dividends are not reinvested; JK's price return ignores the 2023 share issue.
6. **Goodyear India's Q1 FY27 margin** is 4.3% in Q3 (EBITDA basis) and 1.3% on a data site (operating-profit basis); its dividend feed misses 2024 and 2025, understating its TSR by about 1 point a year.
7. **Deck labels:** the region pie in both FY27 decks is captioned "YTD FY22"; the figures are evidently current. "17 quarters" (deck, standalone) and "16 quarters" (CFO, consolidated) are different bases.
8. **FY22 plant capacities** come from a search summary of a page that would not open; they exclude Andhra Pradesh. Do not add them to today's production.
9. **ICRA's FY27 tyre demand** is 5 to 7% in one summary and "1-4%" in a press headline (page not read); CRISIL's FY27 margin view is from an undated press summary. Both need the agency's own release before use in C07.
10. **The FY26 raw-material share** (54.6%) mixes three company quarters with ICICI-A's Q3 figure; the European farm "12% of our revenues" refers to the European manufacturing operation, and the Rs 97 per euro conversion is ours.
